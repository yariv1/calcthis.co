How to use the hours calculator

On the Shift tab, type a start time and an end time as digits — 9 or 915, the colon fills itself — and use the AM / PM toggle inside each field. The start starts on AM and the end on PM, so 9 to 5 is 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Add an unpaid break as minutes (30), hours (1h) or a clock range (12:00-12:30), and tap Add a split shift if you worked two stretches in a day. The Week tab does the same for Monday to Sunday and totals the week; Dates counts the hours between two dates and times; Convert turns minutes, decimal hours and h:mm into each other.

How hours worked are calculated

hours worked = end time − start time − unpaid breaks IF THE END IS EARLIER ON THE CLOCK THAN THE START, IT FALLS ON THE NEXT DAY

For 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM with a 30-minute lunch, the span is 8 hours 30 minutes and the hours worked are 8 hours — 8.00 in decimal hours. An overnight shift works the same way: 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM is 8 hours, ending the next morning.

Rounding to the 7-minute rule

Many employers round time to the nearest 5, 6 or 15 minutes. Under Go advanced, pick a rounding rule and the result shows both the exact and the rounded time. With the 15-minute (7-minute) rule, 1–7 minutes round down and 8–14 round up:

Overtime and pay

On the Week tab, Go advanced lets you set a weekly overtime threshold (commonly 40 hours), an optional daily threshold and a multiplier (commonly 1.5). Daily overtime comes off first and weekly overtime applies to what is left, so no hour is counted twice. Nine hours a day for five days is 45 hours; at a $20 rate with overtime after 40 hours that is $800 for the regular hours plus $150 for 5 overtime hours at 1.5×, or $950. Overtime rules vary by employer and place — check yours.

Common questions

How do I calculate hours worked between two times?

Subtract the start time from the end time, then subtract any unpaid break. For 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM with a 30-minute lunch, the span is 8 hours 30 minutes and the hours worked are 8 hours. If the end time is earlier on the clock than the start time, the shift runs overnight and ends the next day, so 10 PM to 6 AM is 8 hours.

How do I convert minutes to decimal hours?

Divide the minutes by 60. 15 minutes is 0.25 hours, 30 minutes is 0.5, 45 minutes is 0.75, and 20 minutes is 0.33. Add the whole hours in front: 7 hours 45 minutes is 7.75 hours. To go back, multiply the decimal part by 60, so 0.2 hours is 12 minutes.

How do I add up my hours for the week?

Use the Week tab: enter a start time, end time and break for each day and the calculator adds the days into a weekly total in hours and minutes and in decimal hours. Nine hours a day for five days is 45 hours.

How is overtime calculated?

Under Go advanced you can set a weekly threshold (commonly 40 hours), an optional daily threshold and a multiplier (commonly 1.5). Hours over the threshold are overtime. At 45 hours in a week with a 40-hour threshold and a $20 rate, 40 hours pay $800 and 5 overtime hours pay 5 × $20 × 1.5 = $150, for $950. Overtime rules differ by employer and location, so check yours.

What is the 7-minute rule?

It is a payroll rounding rule that rounds time to the nearest 15 minutes: 1 to 7 minutes round down to the previous quarter-hour and 8 to 14 minutes round up to the next. A worked time of 7 hours 52 minutes becomes 7:45, and 7 hours 53 minutes becomes 8:00. Choose 15 minutes under Round to in Go advanced.
