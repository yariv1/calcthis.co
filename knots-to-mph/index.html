How to convert knots to mph

A knot is one nautical mile per hour, and a nautical mile is exactly 1,852 meters, so multiply knots by 1.150779 to get miles per hour: 20 kn = 23.015589 mph. In km/h: 20 kn = 37.04 km/h.

To go the other way multiply mph by 0.868976: 60 mph = 52.138575 kn. A quick mental rule: add about 15% to knots to get mph.

Common questions

How many mph is one knot?

1 kn = 1.1507794 mph (rounded), which is 1 kn = 1.852 km/h.

How do I convert knots to mph?

Multiply by 1.150779. 20 kn = 23.015589 mph, 35 kn = 40.277281 mph and 60 kn = 69.046767 mph.

How do I convert mph to knots?

Multiply by 0.868976. 25 mph = 21.724406 kn, 60 mph = 52.138575 kn and 70 mph = 60.828337 kn.

How fast is 120 knots in mph?

120 kn = 138.09353 mph — a typical small-aircraft cruise speed. A 30-knot wind is 30 kn = 34.523383 mph.

What is a nautical mile?

Exactly 1,852 meters: 1 nmi = 1.1507794 mi and 1 nmi = 1.852 km.
