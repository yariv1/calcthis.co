How to convert volume

Volume units are defined from the liter. The US gallon is exactly 231 cubic inches, or 3.785411784 liters, and the imperial gallon is exactly 4.54609 liters — about 20% bigger. US measures are built from the gallon: 4 quarts, 8 pints, 16 cups, 128 fluid ounces, 256 tablespoons. Example: 5 gal = 18.927059 L, but 5 imp gal = 22.73045 L.

The formula

L = US gallons × 3.785411784ML = US CUPS × 236.5882365 · US GAL = 4 QT = 8 PT = 16 CUPS = 128 FL OZ

Common questions

How many liters are in a gallon?

A US gallon is 3.785411784 L; an imperial (UK) gallon is 4.54609 L. 10 gal = 37.854118 L.

How many cups are in a gallon?

16 US cups: 1 gal = 16 cup. A gallon is also 1 gal = 4 qt, 1 gal = 8 pt and 1 gal = 128 fl oz.

How many mL are in a cup?

A US cup is 236.58824 mL. Metric cups used in the UK, Canada and Australia are 250 mL. 1 cup = 8 fl oz, 1 cup = 16 tbsp.

What is the difference between US and imperial pints?

A US pint is 473.17647 mL and an imperial pint is 568.26125 mL. A US fluid ounce is 29.57353 mL, an imperial one 28.413062 mL.

How do I convert liters to gallons?

Divide liters by 3.785411784 for US gallons: 10 L = 2.6417205 gal. For imperial gallons divide by 4.54609: 10 L = 2.1996925 imp gal.
