How to use the fraction calculator

Type each value into its own row — a fraction like 3/4, a mixed number like 1 3/4, a decimal like 0.125, or a whole number. Pick an operator (+ − × ÷ or of) between the rows, and the answer appears instantly as a simplified fraction, a mixed number, a decimal and a percent, with a bar diagram of the operation and step-by-step working underneath.

Need more than two fractions? Tap Add fraction. Multiplication and division are done before addition and subtraction, exactly like ordinary arithmetic. Switch to Simplify / convert to reduce a single fraction or turn a decimal into a fraction.

How to add fractions

a/b + c/d = (a×d + c×b) / (b×d) FIND A COMMON DENOMINATOR, THEN ADD THE NUMERATORS

Fractions can only be added when they are cut into the same size pieces. Find the least common denominator (LCD), rewrite each fraction over it, then add the tops. For 3/4 + 1/6 the LCD is 12: 9/12 + 2/12 = 11/12. The bar diagram shows exactly this — both fractions re-sliced into twelfths before they are combined. Subtracting works the same way.

How to subtract fractions

Use the same common denominator, then subtract the numerators instead of adding them: 3/4 − 1/6 = 9/12 − 2/12 = 7/12. If the answer is negative, the calculator shows it as a negative fraction.

How to multiply and divide fractions

To multiply, multiply the numerators together and the denominators together, then simplify: 2/3 × 3/5 = 6/15 = 2/5. To divide, flip the second fraction (take its reciprocal) and multiply: 3/4 ÷ 3/8 = 3/4 × 8/3 = 2. You never need a common denominator for these two.

Simplify (reduce) a fraction

Divide the top and bottom by their greatest common factor. 18/24 has a greatest common factor of 6, so it reduces to 3/4. A fraction is in lowest terms when nothing but 1 divides both numbers.

Mixed numbers and improper fractions

An improper fraction has a numerator larger than its denominator, like 7/4. A mixed number writes the same amount as a whole number plus a proper fraction: 7/4 = 1 3/4. To convert a mixed number back, multiply the whole part by the denominator and add the numerator: 1 3/4 = (1×4 + 3)/4 = 7/4. Enter either form and the calculator handles it.

Decimal to fraction

Write the decimal digits over a power of ten, then simplify: 0.125 = 125/1000 = 1/8. Switch to Simplify / convert, type the decimal, and you get the fraction, the mixed number and the percent at once.

Add three or more fractions

Add as many rows as you like. To add 1/2 + 1/3 + 1/4, the LCD of 2, 3 and 4 is 12, so the sum is 6/12 + 4/12 + 3/12 = 13/12 = 1 1/12.

Common questions

How do you add fractions with different denominators?

Find a common denominator (the least common multiple of the denominators), rewrite each fraction over it by multiplying the top and bottom by the same number, then add the numerators and keep the denominator. For 3/4 + 1/6 the common denominator is 12: 9/12 + 2/12 = 11/12.

How do you convert a decimal to a fraction?

Write the decimal digits over a power of ten with the same number of zeros as decimal places, then simplify. For example 0.125 = 125/1000, and dividing the top and bottom by 125 gives 1/8.

How do you simplify (reduce) a fraction?

Divide the numerator and denominator by their greatest common factor. For 18/24 the greatest common factor is 6, so 18/24 = 3/4. A fraction is in lowest terms when the only common factor is 1.

How do you multiply and divide fractions?

To multiply, multiply the numerators together and the denominators together, then simplify. To divide, flip the second fraction (take its reciprocal) and multiply: 3/4 ÷ 3/8 = 3/4 × 8/3 = 2.

Can I add or subtract more than two fractions at once?

Yes. Use Add fraction to add as many rows as you need and pick an operator between each. Multiplication and division are done before addition and subtraction, just like normal arithmetic.
