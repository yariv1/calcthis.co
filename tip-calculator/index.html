How to calculate a tip

Multiply the bill by the tip percentage, then add the result to the bill. A 20% tip on $86.40 is $17.28, so the total is $103.68.

Tip = bill × tip % ÷ 100 TOTAL = BILL + TIP

In your head: 10% is the bill with the decimal point moved one place left ($8.64 on $86.40). 15% is 10% plus half of it ($12.96). 20% is 10% doubled ($17.28). 25% is a quarter of the bill ($21.60).

Tip before tax or on the total?

Both are common. Tipping on the pre-tax amount is the traditional way; tipping on the total is simpler and a little more generous. On a $108.00 bill that includes $8.00 of tax, a 20% tip is $20.00 before tax and $21.60 on the total. Under Go advanced enter the tax and the calculator shows both.

Splitting the bill

Choose the number of people and the calculator divides the total, including the tip, and shows what each person pays. When the total does not divide into whole cents the shares differ by a cent, and they always add up exactly: $101.95 between four is $25.49, $25.49, $25.49 and $25.48.

To split by what each person ordered, tick Split unevenly under Go advanced and enter each person’s amount. Everyone pays their share of the bill and the tip: $32.00 of a $100.00 bill with a 20% tip is $38.40.

Common questions about tipping

How much should I tip at a restaurant?

In the U.S. a tip of 15% to 20% of the bill is typical at restaurants and bars. On a $86.40 bill that is $12.96 at 15%, $15.55 at 18% and $17.28 at 20%. Tipping is voluntary and customs differ by country; the calculator shows the tip at every common percentage at once.

How do I calculate a 15% tip in my head?

Work out 10% by moving the decimal point one place to the left, then add half of that. For a $86.40 bill, 10% is $8.64, half of that is $4.32, so 15% is about $12.96.

How do I calculate a 20% tip in my head?

Work out 10% and double it. On $86.40, 10% is $8.64, so 20% is $17.28. For 18%, take 20% and subtract a tenth of it ($17.28 minus $1.73) to get about $15.55.

Do you tip on the total with tax or before tax?

Both are common. Tipping on the pre-tax amount is the traditional way; tipping on the total is simpler and a little more generous. On a $108.00 bill that includes $8.00 of tax, a 20% tip is $20.00 before tax and $21.60 on the total — $1.60 more. Enter the tax under Go advanced and the calculator shows both side by side.

How do I split a bill and the tip between people?

Add the tip to the bill and divide by the number of people. A $86.40 bill with an 18% tip is $101.95, so four people pay $25.49 each. When the total does not divide into whole cents the shares differ by a cent — here three people pay $25.49 and one pays $25.48 — and the calculator makes them add up exactly. To split by what each person ordered, tick “Split unevenly” under Go advanced: on a $100.00 bill with a 20% tip, a person who ordered $32.00 of $100.00 pays $38.40.

How do I find out what percent I tipped?

Switch the calculator to “What % did I tip?”, enter the bill and the total you paid. If the bill was $86.40 and you paid $102.00, the tip was $15.60, which is 18.06% of the bill.

Should I round the tip up?

Rounding up is a common shortcut. An 18% tip on $86.40 is $15.55; rounded up to a whole dollar it is $16.00, which is really 18.52%. Rounding the whole total up to $102.00 gives a $15.60 tip (18.06%). The calculator always rounds up and shows the real percentage you end up tipping.

How much do I tip for delivery, a haircut or a taxi?

The table above lists typical amounts for the common services. Delivery, hairstylists, barbers, massage and taxi drivers are usually 10% to 20%; hotel housekeeping, shuttle drivers and tour guides are usually a few dollars. These are customs, not rules, and they vary by country and place.
