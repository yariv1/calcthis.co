How to use the commission calculator

With Percent of sales selected, type any two of the three boxes — sales, commission rate or commission — and the third fills in. The line under the boxes says which two were used. Type a third value and it replaces the oldest one, never the sales.

Paid on tiers? Choose Tiered — each slice at its own rate (each slice of your sales earns the rate of its tier) or Tiered — whole sale at the reached rate (the tier your total reaches sets the rate for all of it), type each tier's limit and a percent or a flat amount, and the bar shows every slice. Flat amount per item sold multiplies the items by your amount.

Open Go advanced to add more sales, a base pay, your share of a split, fees, a fixed deduction, who pays the commission and a take-home goal — the sales you need to reach it.

The formulas

commission = sales × commission rate ÷ 100 RATE = COMMISSION ÷ SALES × 100

With a base pay: total pay = base + commission. Tiered, each slice: commission = Σ (sales inside the tier × its rate). Examples, all worked out by this calculator:

If the same plan paid the rate of the tier you reach on all sales, the $27,000.00 would earn 10% of everything: $2,700.00. Plans differ in this, so check your agreement and compare both in the calculator.

From commission to take-home

Your pay is often less than the commission. A $6,000.00 commission with a 70% share, 2% fees and no other deduction leaves $4,116.00: $4,200.00 after the split minus $84.00 in fees. Add a base pay and the total pay shows next to it.

How much do I need to sell?

Type what you want to take home. At 3% with nothing deducted, $5,000.00 takes $166,666.67 of sales. For tiers the calculator searches your tiers for the smallest sales that reach the goal.

Common questions

How do I calculate commission?

Multiply the sales by the commission rate and divide by 100. 3% of $200,000.00 is $6,000.00. In the calculator, type the sales and the rate and the commission fills in.

How do I find the commission rate?

Commission rate = commission ÷ sales × 100. A commission of $1,500.00 on $50,000.00 of sales is 3%. Type the sales and the commission and the rate appears.

How does a tiered commission work?

Each slice of your sales earns the rate of the tier it falls in. With 3% up to $20,000.00, 5% from there to $25,000.00 and 10% above, sales of $27,000.00 earn $1,050.00: only the $2,000.00 above $25,000.00 earns 10%. Choose “Tiered — each slice at its own rate”, type the limits and rates and the bar shows every slice.

What if the whole sale is paid at the rate of the tier I reach?

Some plans pay one rate on all your sales, decided by the tier your total reaches. The same $27,000.00 of sales then earns 10% of everything, which is $2,700.00. Choose “Tiered — whole sale at the reached rate” to compare the two plans on the same sales.

How do I add a base salary to commission?

Open Go advanced and type the base pay. It is added after the commission and any deductions: $500.00 base plus 1.5% of $25,000.00 is $875.00 in total pay.

How do I work out my take-home after a split and fees?

Open Go advanced. Type your share of the commission, the fees as a percent of your share and any fixed deduction. A $6,000.00 commission with a 70% share and 2% fees leaves you $4,116.00 ($4,200.00 share, $84.00 fees).

How much do I need to sell to earn a goal?

Type the amount you want to take home under “Take-home goal” in Go advanced. At 3% with nothing else deducted, $5,000.00 takes $166,666.67 of sales. With tiers, the calculator finds the smallest sales that reach the goal.

Who pays the commission, the seller or the buyer?

Usually the seller: the commission comes out of the price, so the seller keeps the price minus the commission. Sometimes the buyer pays it on top, so the buyer pays the price plus the commission. Choose which in Go advanced and the page shows both totals.

Can I add several sales together?

Yes. Open Go advanced and use “More sales” to add up to six extra sales; they are added to the sales above and the tiers, if you use them, work on the total.

Does the currency selector convert my numbers?

No. It only changes the symbol and the decimals shown. Type your numbers in the currency you use.
