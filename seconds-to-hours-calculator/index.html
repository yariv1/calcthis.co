How to use the seconds to hours calculator

Start with Seconds: the Hours box fills in while you type, and the large figure above is that same time as decimal hours. The plain-words line below it and the clock ring show where inside the final hour you are. Start with the hours instead and the seconds box answers.

Not only digits work: 1:30:00, 1h 30m and 90 min are accepted, and a short line under the boxes shows how your entry was understood. Go advanced opens a box for a whole list of times with a total, and extra units such as weeks, months and years.

The formula

hours = seconds ÷ 3,600 DECIMAL HOURS = SECONDS ÷ 3,600

7,260 seconds ÷ 3,600 = 2.016667 hours. For the hours, minutes and seconds form: 7,260 ÷ 3,600 gives 2 whole hours (2 × 3,600 = 7,200 seconds), the 60 seconds left over are 1 minute and 0 seconds, so 7,260 seconds is 2 hours 1 minute (2:01:00).

Seconds in a day, week, month and year

  • 1 minute: 60 seconds
  • 1 hour: 3,600 seconds
  • 1 day: 86,400 seconds (24 hours)
  • 1 week: 604,800 seconds
  • 30-day month: 2,592,000 seconds
  • 365-day year: 31,536,000 seconds

  • Every hour counts as exactly 3,600 seconds here, so daylight saving changes and leap seconds are ignored. Decimal results show up to six places, and the h:mm:ss form is exact to the millisecond.

Common questions

How many seconds are in an hour?

There are 3,600 seconds in an hour: 60 minutes × 60 seconds. A day has 86,400 seconds, a week 604,800, a 30-day month 2,592,000 and a 365-day year 31,536,000.

How do I convert seconds to hours?

Divide the seconds by 3,600. 5,400 seconds ÷ 3,600 = 1.5 hours. In the calculator, type the seconds in the first box and the hours appear in the second.

How many hours is 86,400 seconds?

86,400 seconds is 24 hours, exactly one day (24:00:00).

How many hours is 7,200 seconds?

7,200 seconds is 2 hours (2:00:00).

How do I convert seconds to hours, minutes and seconds?

Take the whole hours from seconds ÷ 3,600, then turn what is left into minutes and seconds. 7,260 seconds is 2.016667 hours, which is 2 hours 1 minute: 2 × 3,600 = 7,200, and the 60 seconds left over make 1 minute. The calculator shows this as 2:01:00.

How do I convert hours to seconds?

Multiply the hours by 3,600. 3 hours is 10,800 seconds and 24 hours is 86,400 seconds. In the calculator, type the hours in the second box and the seconds appear in the first.

How do I total a list of times?

Open Go advanced and paste the values, one per line or separated by semicolons. 5400, 7200 and 1:30:00 give 18,000 seconds in all, which is 5 hours; each line also gets its own row in a small table.

How do I do it in Excel or Google Sheets?

For decimal hours divide by 3,600: if the seconds are in A1, type =A1/3600. For a clock time divide by 86,400 (the seconds in a day) and format the cell as [h]:mm:ss.

Does it take daylight saving or leap seconds into account?

No. Every hour is counted as 3,600 seconds and every day as 86,400 seconds, which is how clocks and timesheets add durations.
