How to use the seconds to minutes calculator

Put the Seconds in and the Minutes appear as a decimal alongside; below the big number sits the same time as minutes and seconds, and the ring treats one minute as a full circle so you can see the extra seconds. Typing minutes works in reverse.

Stopwatch style input works: 1:30 (minutes and seconds), 1m 30s or 90 sec. The line under the boxes shows how the entry was read. Go advanced takes a list of lap or clip times, one per line, and adds them up.

The formula

minutes = seconds ÷ 60 DECIMAL MINUTES = SECONDS ÷ 60

1,000 seconds ÷ 60 = 16.666667 minutes. For the minutes and seconds form: 1,000 ÷ 60 gives 16 whole minutes (16 × 60 = 960 seconds) and 40 seconds left over, so 1,000 seconds is 16 minutes 40 seconds (16:40).

Timing, laps and clips

Stopwatch laps, video clips and song lengths are usually written as minutes and seconds. The "Minutes:seconds" line of the calculator is that form, so 1,000 seconds reads as 16:40. Paste a list of lap or clip lengths under Go advanced to get the total.

Common questions

How many seconds are in a minute?

There are 60 seconds in a minute. An hour has 3,600 seconds and a day 86,400.

How do I convert seconds to minutes?

Divide the seconds by 60. 90 seconds ÷ 60 = 1.5 minutes. In the calculator, type the seconds in the first box and the minutes appear in the second.

How many minutes is 300 seconds?

300 seconds is 5 minutes (5:00).

How many minutes is 3,600 seconds?

3,600 seconds is 60 minutes, which is 1 hour.

How many minutes is 1,000 seconds?

1,000 seconds is 16.666667 minutes, which is 16 minutes 40 seconds (16:40).

How many minutes is 500 seconds?

500 seconds is 8.333333 minutes, which is 8 minutes 20 seconds (8:20).

How many minutes is 900, 1,200 and 1,800 seconds?

900 seconds is 15 minutes, 1,200 seconds is 20 minutes and 1,800 seconds is 30 minutes.

How do I convert seconds to minutes and seconds?

Take the whole minutes from seconds ÷ 60 and keep the remainder as seconds. 1,000 seconds ÷ 60 gives 16 whole minutes (16 × 60 = 960) with 40 seconds left, so 1,000 seconds is 16 minutes 40 seconds.

How do I convert minutes to seconds?

Multiply the minutes by 60. 5 minutes is 300 seconds and 15 minutes is 900 seconds. In the calculator, type the minutes in the second box and the seconds appear in the first.

How do I add up lap or clip times?

In Go advanced type one time per line: 45, 90 and 1:15 total 210 seconds, which is 3.5 minutes (3 minutes 30 seconds).
