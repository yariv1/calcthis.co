How to use the time average calculator

Choose Durations or Clock times, then type or paste one time per line (or separate them with commas). Durations can be written as 1:30:45, 2h 15m, 45 min, 95 s or a bare number. Set "Read 1:30 as" to h:mm (hours and minutes) or m:ss (minutes and seconds, handy for lap times).

You get the average, the total, the median, the shortest and longest, the average as decimal hours, minutes and seconds, and a bar for every time with the average marked. Under Go advanced you can round the average and see every time compared with it.

The average time formula

Average time = total time ÷ number of times CONVERT EVERYTHING TO SECONDS, ADD, DIVIDE, CONVERT BACK

1:30:45 is 5445 seconds, 2h 15m is 8100, 45 min is 2700 and 95 s is 95. Together they are 16340 seconds (4:32:20), and 16340 ÷ 4 = 4085 seconds, which is 1:08:05.

Averaging clock times across midnight

Clock times are not ordinary numbers: 11:30 PM and 12:30 AM are only an hour apart, but a plain average of the clock numbers puts them at about noon. The calculator treats the clock as a circle and averages the direction each time points to, so it handles midnight correctly.

For 11:30 pm, 12:15 am, 11:45 pm, 12:30 am the average is 12:00 AM — a plain average would give 12:00 PM. For 10:30 PM, 11:15 PM, 11:45 PM and 12:30 AM the average is 11:30 PM. You also get the earliest and latest time relative to the average and the total spread — useful for average bedtimes, wake-up times or arrival times.

Durations vs clock times

  • Durations are lengths of time (a 1:30:45 run, a 45-minute meeting). They add up, so the average is simply the total divided by the count.
  • Clock times are points on the day (11:30 PM). They wrap around, so the average needs the circular method above.

Times without am/pm count as AM or PM as you choose; 24-hour times such as 23:30 are read as they are. To add or subtract times use the time calculator, to total a work week the hours calculator, and for the average of any other numbers the average calculator.

Common questions

How do you calculate the average of several times?

Convert each time to seconds, add them up, divide by how many there are, and convert back. 1:30:45, 2h 15m, 45 min and 95 s total 4:32:20, and 4:32:20 ÷ 4 = 1:08:05.

What time formats can I enter?

Durations: 1:30:45, 1:30 (read as h:mm or m:ss — you choose), 2h 15m, 45 min, 95 s, 1.5h, or a bare number (minutes in h:mm mode, seconds in m:ss mode). Clock times: 11:30 pm, 11pm, 23:30, 0:15.

How do I average bedtimes that cross midnight?

Switch to Clock times and enter them. The calculator averages the position on the clock rather than the numbers, so 11:30 PM and 12:30 AM average to midnight, not noon.

What is the difference between average time and total time?

Total time is all the times added together; average time is that total divided by the number of times. Both are shown, along with the median, shortest and longest.

Can I average lap or race times?

Yes. Set "Read 1:30 as" to m:ss so that 1:30 means 1 minute 30 seconds, then list the lap times. Decimal seconds such as 1:30.25 also work.

How do I convert the average to hours or minutes?

The table shows the average as decimal hours, minutes and seconds — for example 1.1347 hours or 68.083 minutes for the example list.

Can I average times that are spread all around the clock?

If the times point evenly around the day (for example 12:00 AM, 6:00 AM, 12:00 PM and 6:00 PM) there is no meaningful average, and the calculator says so rather than showing a misleading time.
