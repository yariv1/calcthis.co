How to convert cm to inches

An inch is defined as exactly 2.54 centimeters, so the conversion is one division. Divide the centimeters by 2.54: 50 cm = 19.685039 in. On a tape measure that is 19 11/16 inches, to the nearest 1/16.

To go the other way, multiply inches by 2.54: 12 in = 30.48 cm. The swap button flips the direction.

The formula

inches = cm ÷ 2.54CM = INCHES × 2.54 · 1 INCH = EXACTLY 2.54 CM

Common questions

How many inches are in a centimeter?

1 cm = 0.3937007874 in (rounded). Because an inch is exactly 2.54 cm, dividing by 2.54 is always exact.

How do I convert cm to inches?

Divide by 2.54. 25 cm = 9.8425197 in, 30 cm = 11.811024 in and 60 cm = 23.622047 in.

How do I convert inches to cm?

Multiply by 2.54. 1 in = 2.54 cm, 12 in = 30.48 cm and 36 in = 91.44 cm.

What is 180 cm in feet and inches?

180 cm = 70.866142 in, which is 5 ft 10 7/8 in. The converter shows the feet-and-inches split under the answer when you convert to feet.

How do I read centimeters as a fraction of an inch?

Choose inches as the result and the answer is also shown as a tape-measure fraction, rounded to the nearest 1/16 in: 15 cm = 5.9055118 in is 5 7/8 in.
