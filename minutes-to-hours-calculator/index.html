How to use the minutes to hours calculator

Enter your Minutes and read decimal Hours straight away: 45 minutes becomes 0.75, 90 becomes 1.5. The plain-words line and the clock face show the leftover inside the last hour, and typing hours instead gives you the minutes.

Timesheet formats work too: 8h 30m or 2:15 (hours and minutes) are read for you, and a line under the boxes confirms how. In Go advanced pick rounding to the nearest 5, 6 or 15 minutes, paste a column of durations for a total, or see weeks, months and years.

The formula

hours = minutes ÷ 60 DECIMAL HOURS = WHOLE HOURS + MINUTES ÷ 60

150 minutes ÷ 60 = 2.5 hours. For the hours and minutes form: 150 ÷ 60 gives 2 whole hours (2 × 60 = 120 minutes) and 30 minutes left over, so 150 minutes is 2 hours 30 minutes (2:30:00). And 8 hours 30 minutes as decimal hours: 8 + 30 ÷ 60 = 8.5.

The mistake to avoid

The digits after the decimal point are a fraction of an hour, not minutes: 7.5 hours is 7 hours 30 minutes (7:30), not 7:50, and 8:45 is 8.75 hours, not 8.45. The calculator shows both forms side by side so you can copy the one your form asks for.

Rounding for timesheets

Open Go advanced and choose the nearest 5, 6 or 15 minutes. For 100 minutes the nearest 15 is 105 minutes (1.75 hours). This page only does the arithmetic: use the rounding rule your employer or payroll system states.

Common questions

How do I convert minutes to hours?

Divide the minutes by 60. 90 minutes ÷ 60 = 1.5 hours. In the calculator, type the minutes in the first box and the hours appear in the second.

How many hours is 150 minutes?

150 minutes is 2.5 hours, or 2 hours 30 minutes (2:30:00).

How many hours is 180 minutes?

180 minutes is 3 hours (3:00:00).

How many hours is 300 minutes?

300 minutes is 5 hours (5:00:00).

How many hours is 1,000 minutes?

1,000 minutes is 16.666667 hours, which is 16 hours 40 minutes (16:40:00).

How many hours is 115 minutes?

115 minutes is 1.916667 hours, which is 1 hour 55 minutes (1:55:00).

How do I convert hours and minutes to decimal hours?

Add the minutes divided by 60 to the whole hours: 8 hours 30 minutes is 8 + 30 ÷ 60 = 8.5 hours. In the calculator type 8h 30m (or 510) in the minutes box and read 8.5 in the hours box.

Why is 7.5 hours not 7:50?

Because the part after the decimal point is a fraction of an hour, not minutes. 7.5 hours is 7 hours and 30 minutes (7:30); 7:50 is 7 hours 50 minutes, which is 7.833333 decimal hours.

How do I round minutes to the nearest 15, 6 or 5 for a timesheet?

Open Go advanced and pick a rounding. For example 100 minutes to the nearest 15 minutes is 105 minutes, which is 1.75 hours. Rounding to 6 minutes gives tenths of an hour. Use whichever rule your employer states.

How do I total a column of durations?

In Go advanced paste one value per line. 45, 90 and 1:30 come to 225 minutes, or 3.75 hours, with each line converted next to it.
