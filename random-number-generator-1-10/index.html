Both 1 and 10 can come up — that is 10 possible numbers, each equally likely.

Change the range any time, or ask for many numbers at once with No repeats.

Common questions

How do I get a random number between 1 and 10?

Press Generate. Both 1 and 10 are included, so each of the 10 numbers has a 1 in 10 chance (10%).

Can I get several numbers from 1 to 10 with no repeats?

Yes — tick No repeats and set “How many numbers”. You can ask for up to 10, which returns every number from 1 to 10 in random order.

What are the odds of the same number twice in a row?

It is 1 in 10 (10%). Each draw is independent, so a number that just came up is no more or less likely next time.

How do I draw 1 to 10 without repeating?

Switch to Draw one at a time. Each draw removes the number from the pile, so after ten draws you have dealt every number from 1 to 10 exactly once.

Is this random number generator truly random?

The numbers come from your browser’s secure random source (the same one used for encryption keys), and the range maths is exact, so no number is favoured. It is a computer-generated source rather than hardware noise, which is more than enough for games, draws, raffles, sampling and classroom use.
