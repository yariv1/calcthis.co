Calculators that do one thing well

Most calculator sites are enormous libraries where every tool is a compromise — a generic form bolted onto a shared template. CalcThis takes the opposite approach. Each calculator here is designed around a single real-world task, so the inputs match how you actually measure, the results come out in the units your supplier sells by, and the whole thing works the instant you type — no compute button, no account, no clutter.

Today the hub covers eight of the most-searched estimating jobs in construction and landscaping: figuring out board feet and lumber cost for a cut list, working out how much gravel a driveway or path needs in tons or bags, how much sand a paver base or leveling job takes, how much topsoil a lawn or raised bed needs by the cubic yard or bag, estimating mulch for garden beds, working out how many bags of concrete a slab or footing takes, how many boxes of flooring a room needs, and exactly how many tiles a floor, wall or backsplash takes. Each construction tool includes a running project total so you can add several boards, areas or beds and price the whole job at once — plus a waste factor so you order enough the first time. The ninth calculator opens a new category — fitness — with a running pace tool that solves pace, time or distance and predicts your finish times.

Why single-purpose beats all-in-one

A dedicated tool can make choices a general calculator can't: sensible defaults, the exact depth options people really use, unit handling that matches the trade, and a layout tuned for one job on a phone at the lumber yard or garden centre. That focus is the whole point — fast, accurate, and purpose-built, one calculator at a time.
