How to use the cat age calculator

Type your cat’s age in years and months — or, under Go advanced, a date of birth. You get the age in human years, the life stage, a timeline with your cat marked on it, the age on the next birthday and how long until your cat is a senior. Switch to Human → cat to work the other way.

The 15-9-4 rule

1st year = 15 · 2nd year = +9 · every year after = +4 CAT YEARS TO HUMAN YEARS

So 2 years is 24, 5 years is 36 (24 + 3 × 4), 10 years is 56 and 15 years is 76. For kittens the calculator uses the published early-life points — 3 months is 4, 6 months 10, 12 months 15 and 18 months 21 — with straight lines between them.

Common questions

How do I calculate my cat’s age in human years?

Use the 15-9-4 rule: the first year of a cat’s life counts as 15 human years, the second adds 9 more (24 at age 2), and every year after that adds 4. A 5-year-old cat is 36, a 10-year-old 56 and a 15-year-old 76 in human years. Enter the age above and the calculator does it, including months.

Is one cat year really 7 human years?

No. The “×7” rule is wrong at both ends: a 1-year-old cat is about 15 in human terms (not 7) and a 10-year-old cat is about 56 (not 70). The calculator shows the ×7 figure beside the real one so you can see the gap.

How old is a 6-month-old cat in human years?

About 10, and a 3-month-old kitten about 4. Kittens grow fastest early on, which is why the calculator uses the published early-life points (3 months = 4, 6 months = 10, 12 months = 15, 18 months = 21) instead of a straight line from zero.

How old is a 1-year-old or 2-year-old cat in human years?

A 1-year-old cat is about 15 and a 2-year-old about 24 (15 + 9). After that, each year adds 4: a 3-year-old is 28 and a 4-year-old 32.

What are the life stages of a cat?

The AAHA/AAFP 2021 Feline Life Stage Guidelines use four age-based stages: kitten (birth to 1 year), young adult (1 to 6 years), mature adult (7 to 10 years) and senior (over 10 years). Cats 15 and over are often called geriatric, a term from the older six-stage guidelines. The calculator names your cat’s stage and shows how long until the next one.

When is a cat considered a senior?

After 10 years of age — a cat of 11 is about 60 in human years. The calculator shows how long your cat has until then, and for a cat already past 10 it says so.

Can I convert a human age to cat years?

Yes. Switch to “Human → cat” at the top and enter the human age. A 60-year-old equivalent is about 11 years, an 11-year-old cat; the calculator also shows the ×7 comparison.

How do I work out my cat’s age from a birthday?

Open Go advanced and pick or type the date of birth; the age in years and months fills in. For an adopted cat with an unknown birthday, type the age the vet estimated instead.

How accurate is a cat age calculator?

It is an estimate. The 15-9-4 chart is the one used by pet-care organisations and vets, but cats vary with breed, health and lifestyle, and I could not find a single primary table behind it — the sources used are listed on this page. The life stages come from the AAHA/AAFP guidelines.
