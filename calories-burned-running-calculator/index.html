How to use the running calculator

Enter any two of distance, time and pace or speed and the third fills in. Add an incline and your weight. The result shows total calories, the extra over resting, calories per mile or km, and the same time at other speeds. Go advanced adds a calorie goal (time needed), a personalised resting rate and an energy unit switch.

The running calories formula

VO₂ = 0.2 × speed + 0.9 × speed × grade + 3.5 ; calories/min = VO₂ × weight (kg) ÷ 200 ACSM RUNNING EQUATION · SPEED IN m/min, GRADE AS A FRACTION

Running 6 mph (160.9 m/min) on the flat: VO₂ = 0.2 × 160.9 + 3.5 = 35.7 mL/kg/min, or 10.20 METs — 12.5 kcal a minute for a 155 lb person, 753 an hour, 125 a mile.

Common questions

How many calories does running burn?

A 155 lb (70.3 kg) person burns about 753 kcal an hour at 6 mph (10 min/mile) and about 979 at 8 mph (7.5 min/mile), by the ACSM running equation. Enter your own pace and weight above.

How many calories does running a mile burn?

About 125 kcal for a 155 lb person at a 10 min/mile pace, and about 122 at 7.5 min/mile. A mile costs a similar amount at most paces; the pace mostly changes how fast you spend it. The table above gives a mile at several speeds and weights, and the calculator shows “per mile” and “per km” for your numbers.

How do I calculate calories burned running?

The ACSM running equation gives oxygen use: VO₂ = 0.2 × speed + 0.9 × speed × grade + 3.5 (speed in metres a minute, grade as a fraction). Divide by 3.5 for METs, then calories a minute = METs × 3.5 × weight in kg ÷ 200. At 6 mph (161 m/min) flat that is 10.20 METs, which is 12.5 kcal a minute for a 155 lb person.

Does running uphill or on an incline burn more?

Yes. At 6 mph a 155 lb person burns about 753 kcal an hour flat and about 905 at a 5% incline. On a treadmill, enter the incline percent.

What is the difference between total and extra calories?

The total includes calories your body would burn resting during the run. The extra figure subtracts them — it is what the run adds to your day, and the one to use for a calorie deficit.

Is the ACSM running equation accurate for jogging?

The ACSM running equation applies above about 5 mph, or to jogging faster than 3 mph if you are actually jogging. Below 5 mph the calculator still gives a figure but flags it; if you are walking, use the walking calculator.

How accurate is a calories burned running calculator?

It is an estimate: terrain, wind, running economy, fitness and body composition move the real figure by 10% or more. It is a good guide for comparing runs and tracking trends. The advanced option swaps the standard resting rate for one from your age, sex and height.

How many calories do I burn in a 5K or a marathon?

A 5K is 3.107 miles — about 390 kcal for a 155 lb person at 10 min/mile; a marathon (26.2 miles) about 3,287. Enter the distance and your pace to see yours, with the extra over resting beside it.
