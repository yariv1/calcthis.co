How to convert length

Every length unit is defined from the meter, so any conversion is one multiplication. The inch is exactly 2.54 centimeters (the 1959 international yard and pound agreement), the foot is 12 inches, and the mile is 5,280 feet or exactly 1.609344 kilometers. To convert centimeters to inches, divide by 2.54: 180 cm = 70.866142 in, which is 70 7/8 in on a tape measure.

The formula

cm = inches × 2.54INCHES = CM ÷ 2.54 · FEET × 0.3048 = METERS · MILES × 1.609344 = KM

Common questions

How many centimeters are in an inch?

Exactly 2.54. 1 in = 2.54 cm, 12 in = 30.48 cm and 36 in = 91.44 cm. The inch has been defined as 2.54 cm since the international agreement of 1959.

How do I convert cm to inches?

Divide the centimeters by 2.54. 180 cm = 70.866142 in — on a tape measure that is 70 7/8 inches, or 5 ft 10 7/8 in. Choose inches as the result unit and the tape-measure fraction appears under the answer.

How many feet are in a mile?

5,280 feet, or 1,760 yards: 1 mi = 5,280 ft. A mile is exactly 1.609344 kilometers.

How do I convert miles to kilometers?

Multiply miles by 1.609344. 26.2 mi = 42.164813 km for a marathon and 10 km = 6.2137119 mi for a 10K.

What is a nautical mile?

A nautical mile is exactly 1,852 meters, used in sea and air navigation: 1 nmi = 1.1507794 mi and 1 nmi = 1.852 km.
