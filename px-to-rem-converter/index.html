How to convert px to rem

A rem is the root (html) font size, which browsers set to 16 px unless you change it, so 24 px = 1.5 rem and 1.5 rem = 24 px. An em is the font size of the element's parent, so it equals rem only when the parent is the root size. A CSS inch is always 96 px, a point is 1/72 inch: 12 pt = 16 px.

The formula

rem = px ÷ root font sizePX = REM × ROOT FONT SIZE · BROWSERS DEFAULT TO 16 PX · 1 IN = 96 PX · 1 PT = 4/3 PX

Common questions

How do I convert px to rem?

Divide the pixels by the root font size, 16 px by default. 24 px = 1.5 rem, 14 px = 0.875 rem and 10 px = 0.625 rem. If your root size is 10 px, enter 10 in the root font size box above and 24 px becomes 2.4 rem.

How do I convert rem to px?

Multiply rem by the root font size. 1 rem = 16 px, 1.5 rem = 24 px and 2 rem = 32 px.

What is the difference between em and rem?

Rem is relative to the root font size and em to the parent element's font size. 24 px = 1.2 em when the parent is 20 px, but 24 px = 1.5 rem whatever the parent is. Set the parent size under Go advanced to convert em.

How many pixels are in a point, pica and inch?

In CSS: 1 pt = 1.3333333 px, 1 pc = 16 px and 1 in = 96 px. So a 12 pt font is 16 px.

How do I convert px to vw?

Divide the pixels by the viewport width and multiply by 100. On a 1,920 px wide viewport 960 px = 50 vw. Enter your viewport width under Go advanced.
