How to use the turkey size calculator

Enter the number of adults and children and say whether you want leftovers. The calculator plans 1 pound of whole turkey per adult and half a pound per child, multiplies by 1.5 for leftovers, and rounds up to the size to buy. Under Go advanced you can take out guests who are not eating turkey and change the portion sizes.

You also get the thaw and roasting times for that size, and one click carries the weight into the turkey cooking time calculator to plan the whole dinner.

Why a pound per person

A whole turkey is a lot of bone, skin and fat, so a pound of turkey gives each adult a generous serving of meat — about half a pound — with a little left over. Children eat roughly half an adult portion. For turkey sandwiches and casserole the next day, plan on 1.5 pounds per person.

What if the turkey is over 24 lb?

Buy two. Two 14 lb turkeys roast faster than one 28 lb bird, fit in a normal oven, and the USDA roasting and thawing charts stop at 24 lb. The calculator splits large orders into two equal turkeys for you.

Next: thaw it and cook it

A frozen turkey needs days in the refrigerator — the turkey thawing time calculator tells you the day and hour to start — and then roasting time by weight and stuffing in the turkey cooking time calculator.

Common questions

How big a turkey do I need for 10 people?

For 10 adults buy a 10 lb turkey (1 lb per adult), or 15 lb if you want leftovers. Count each child as half an adult: 8 adults and 4 children need 10 lb, or 15 lb with leftovers.

How big a turkey do I need for 8 people?

A 8 lb turkey feeds 8 adults, and 12 lb leaves enough for leftovers.

How big a turkey do I need for 12 people?

12 lb for 12 adults, or 18 lb with leftovers.

How much turkey per person?

A common guideline is 1 pound of whole turkey per adult and half a pound per child, because bones, skin and fat make up much of the weight. For leftovers multiply by 1.5.

What if I need more than 24 lb?

Buy two smaller turkeys. They roast faster, fit a normal oven and the USDA roasting chart stops at 24 lb. For 25 adults with leftovers that is 2 × 19 lb.

How long does it take to thaw and cook the turkey?

A 12 lb turkey takes 2.4 to 3 days to thaw in the refrigerator and 2¾–3 hours to roast unstuffed at 325 °F; a 15 lb turkey takes 3 to 3.8 days and 3¾–4¼ hours. The calculator shows both for the size you buy.
