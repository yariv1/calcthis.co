Car sales tax at a glance

Vehicle tax is not the same as the sales tax on a shirt. Most states tax the price you pay for the car, but each state has its own answer to three questions: does the trade-in come off the taxed price, does a manufacturer rebate, and is the dealer doc fee taxed? The answers change the bill by hundreds of dollars.

On a $30,000 car with a $10,000 trade-in, no rebate and no fees: North Carolina $600.00, Michigan $1,200.00, Virginia $1,245.00, Texas $1,250.00, Georgia $1,400.00. North Carolina’s 3% is the lowest rate here, and Virginia is high for its 4.15% rate because the trade-in does not lower the taxed amount.

How the tax is worked out

Tax = (price + taxed fees − trade-in − rebate) × rate EACH STATE DECIDES WHICH OF THE LAST THREE COUNT
  • 1. Start with the price after any dealer discount.
  • 2. Add the dealer doc fee if your state taxes it.
  • 3. Subtract the trade-in if your state gives a credit, and the rebate if it lets rebates reduce the price.
  • 4. Multiply by the state rate, plus the local rate where there is one.

Common questions about car sales tax

How is car sales tax calculated?

Take the taxed amount — usually the price plus any taxed dealer fee, less whatever your state lets you subtract — and multiply it by the vehicle tax rate. Each state decides what comes off: a trade-in, a manufacturer rebate and the dealer doc fee are treated differently from state to state. On a $30,000 car with a $10,000 trade-in the tax is $600.00 in North Carolina, $1,200.00 in Michigan, $1,245.00 in Virginia, $1,250.00 in Texas and $1,400.00 in Georgia.

Does a trade-in lower the sales tax on a car?

In most states it does, but not everywhere. Texas, Florida, Georgia (dealer sales), North Carolina, Missouri, Arkansas and Arizona take the trade-in off the taxed amount. Michigan does too, up to $12,000 in 2026. California and Virginia do not: on the same $30,000 car with a $10,000 trade-in Virginia still taxes the full $30,000, so the tax is $1,245.00 instead of $830.00.

Are manufacturer rebates taxed?

It depends on the state. In Virginia the price is taken after rebates and incentives, so a $1,000 rebate on a $30,000 car with a $10,000 trade-in lowers the tax from $1,245.00 to $1,203.50. California, Michigan and Arkansas keep the rebate in the taxed amount, and Missouri and Arizona let it reduce the tax. For Texas, Florida, Georgia and North Carolina the official pages we read do not settle it, so the calculator shows the tax both ways instead of guessing.

Is the dealer doc fee taxed?

In California, Florida, Georgia, North Carolina, Virginia and Michigan the doc fee is part of the taxed price. For Texas, Missouri, Arkansas and Arizona the official pages we read do not say, so the calculator shows both results: on a $30,000 car with a $10,000 trade-in, a $500 doc fee and a $1,000 rebate the Texas tax is between $1,187.50 and $1,281.25.

Do I pay tax where I buy the car or where I live?

Where you register it, in the states we have checked: Missouri adds the local tax of the city or county where you live, Florida uses the county of your address on the title, and California uses the rate of the place where the vehicle is registered. Texas collects the 6.25% motor vehicle tax through your county tax office. Florida, for example, charges its own tax on a vehicle bought elsewhere and then titled there, with credit for tax already paid to the other state, so buying in a lower-tax state does not simply avoid it.

Is the title and registration fee taxed?

The calculator adds title and registration fees to your total but does not tax them. Florida’s Department of Revenue states that fees mandated by state law for titling, licensing or registering a vehicle are not subject to tax; check the other states’ rules for your own bill.

How much is the minimum vehicle tax in Virginia?

Virginia charges 4.15% of the gross sales price and its Department of Motor Vehicles says the amount due is based on the price or $75, whichever is greater. On a $1,000 vehicle that is $75.00 rather than $41.50.

What about Florida’s county surtax?

Florida adds your county’s surtax to the 6% state tax on only the first $5,000 of the price. On a $20,000 car in a county at 7% in total the tax is $1,250.00, not $1,400.00. Pick your county or city and the calculator applies the limit.

What if my state is not on your list?

The calculator uses the state’s general sales tax rate and lets you choose whether the trade-in comes off, whether a rebate comes off and whether the doc fee is taxed — because we have not checked that state’s vehicle rules yet. In New York at 4% the same $30,000 car with a $10,000 trade-in is taxed $800.00 if the trade-in comes off and $1,200.00 if it does not. Add your local tax rate for a fuller estimate, and confirm the rules with your state.
