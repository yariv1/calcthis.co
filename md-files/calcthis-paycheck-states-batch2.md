## Sources (official, read 2026-11-02) and what to re-check
- **NJ** — NJ Division of Taxation "Tables for Percentage Method of Withholding" (https://www.nj.gov/treasury/taxation/pdf/NJWithholdingRateTablesPercentageMethod.pdf, tables dated "on and after Oct 1, 2020", still the tables the NJ-WT guide of Sept 2025 points to). Rate A = single / married filing separately, Rate B = joint / head of household (NJ-WT p.24). Allowance $1,000 a year (periodic values 19.20 / 38.40 / 41.60 / 83.30 / 250 / 500 / 2.70). Per-period tables are generated from the annual brackets with the same rounding NJ prints (floors to dollars; bases to the cent for weekly, quarterly, semiannual, annual, daily and Rate B semi-monthly, to the dollar for the other periods) and were checked against the printed bases. Rate C-E (NJ-W4 line 3) are not offered. Employee programs (NJDOL rate page, 2026 worker rates): UI 0.3825% + WF/SWF 0.0425% on $44,800; TDI 0.19% and FLI 0.23% on $171,100.
- **OH** — "Employer Withholding Taxes - Percentage Method", effective Aug 1 2026 (dam.assets.ohio.gov ... WHT_PercentageMethod_2026.pdf): 1.6% / 2.99% / 3.4%, exemption per period; top rate 3.4% confirmed by EY Tax News 2026-1619. Quarterly / semiannual / annual are derived from the annual equivalent of the same table ($650 exemption, brackets 26,050 and 100,000). School-district and city taxes are NOT included (typed local % under Go advanced).
- **MA** — mass.gov "Withholding Tax Guide" (posted Aug 2026): FICA deduction up to $2,000 a year (applied per period as 2,000 / periods), exemption amount = per-exemption + base (weekly 19 + 66 ... annual 1,000 + 3,400, zero if no exemption), 5%, 9% above $1,107,750 (Circular M p.12 via search summary), head-of-household reduction (weekly 2.31 ... monthly 10.00). The Circular M PDF itself is blocked to our tools. PFML 0.46% employee maximum (mass.gov DFML rates page) up to the SS wage base. Blind exemption not offered.
- **MD** — Comptroller 2026 Withholding Guide (percentage charts for 10 local rates 2.25-3.30%, standard deduction $3,400, exemption $3,200, do-not-withhold floors) + "2026 Maryland State and Local Income Tax Withholding Information" memo (county rates, 3.30% default when no county). Chart picked = smallest chart rate >= the county rate (guide step 4). Anne Arundel / Frederick: local rate steps by taxable income; the step that applies to the employee's annual taxable pay is used (our reading of the guide, flagged on the page). Head of household uses the joint chart (guide). Nonresident / Delaware-resident tables not offered. Chart bases differ from our generated ones by up to 4 cents.
- **WI** — Pub W-166 (1/26) "Alternate Method of Withholding" (sliding standard deduction, $400 per exemption, 3.54 / 4.65 / 5.30 / 7.65%): the three worked examples in the guide match to the cent. Head of household uses the single method (the guide only has single and married).
- **IN** — Departmental Notice #1 effective Oct 1 2026 (R47 / 10-26): state 2.95%, county rates for all 92 counties, exemption tables (worked example matches). Next notice: new rates Jan 1 2027 (refresh the 92 rows + 2.95% if it changes).
- **OR** — 150-206-436 Oregon Withholding Tax Formulas (Rev. 12-31-25): formulas implemented as printed (federal-tax subtraction cap $8,750 with phase-out; the same document's FAQ #3 still says $8,500 — the formulas say 8,750). Paid Leave Oregon 0.6% (60% of 1%) up to $184,500 (paidleave.oregon.gov, via search) and Statewide Transit Tax 0.1% (oregon.gov/dor, Measure 120 failed May 19 2026 so the rate continues). Workers' Benefit Fund (cents per hour) not included; Multnomah / Metro taxes not included.
- **UT** — Publication 14 (rev. June 1 2026): 4.45%, base allowance $485 single / $970 married reduced by 1.3% of pay above $9,348 / $18,696. The Pub 14 PDF is blocked to our tools; figures come from the NFC (USDA) payroll bulletin NFC-26-1782763921 (July 6 2026) which restates the formula — re-verify against Pub 14 when reachable.
