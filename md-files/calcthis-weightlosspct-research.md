## Proposed differentiators
Visual: progress bar from start to goal with tick marks at 3%, 5% and 10% of body weight (sources below) and a marker where you are.
Functional: (1) any-direction solving; (2) percent of goal reached + percent still to go, in your units; (3) "what 3/5/10/15/20% means for YOUR weight" table (kg/lb lost and the weight you land on; click to set as goal); (4) Go advanced: start date + today -> average loss a week and as % of body weight a week vs the CDC 1-2 lb range, and the date you reach goal at that pace; (5) % of a typical 5-10% target reached.

## Sources READ (reuse; both already cited on /weight-loss-calculator/)

- NHLBI "Aim for a Healthy Weight": 5% to 10% of initial weight over about 6 months; 3-5% lowers triglycerides and glucose.
