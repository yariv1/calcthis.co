## What nobody does (differentiators)
- **Visual:** a to-scale flask: amber stock layer + water layer up to the final mark, the ratio written on it (1 part + 3 parts = 4 parts), plus a strength bar from the stock down to the target. None of the competitors draws anything.
- **Functional:** (1) type any two of the strength / volume / ratio / factor facts and the rest fill in (competitors need a "solve for" pick); (2) the target accepts every way people write it: 1:10, 1 in 10, 10x, 5%, 250 ppm, 2 mM; (3) the ratio ambiguity is spelled out (1:3 = 1 part + 3 parts = 1 in 4 = factor 4) so nobody mixes it up; (4) a batch-size picker in cups / litres / gallons with the order of mixing written out (water first, add the concentrate, top up); (5) one batch, many strengths: a table of stock and water for 1:2, 1:5, 1:10, 1:50, 1:100 at the same batch size; (6) refuses an impossible target with a plain reason (target stronger than the stock).
