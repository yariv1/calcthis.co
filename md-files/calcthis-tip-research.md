**Sources read:** JAPAN — JNTO "Tipping" page https://www.japan.travel/en/plan/tipping-in-japan/ (no date): not common to tip in bars, cafes, restaurants, taxis, hotels; "tipping is not expected" even in the exceptions; a private guide or interpreter "accustomed to overseas practices" may accept a tip as a gesture of gratitude; custom "kokorozuke" in some situations; if you tip, put it in an envelope, discreetly (envelopes at convenience / stationery / 100 yen stores). ITALY — TravelAwaits "Tipping in Italy" (Catherine Ann Lombard, Apr 1 2023; NO official source found): tipping uncommon, cash; coperto EUR1-3 per person is NOT a tip, pane EUR1-2; servizio ~15-20% often included so no extra needed; otherwise round up (to nearest EUR10 for a good meal); bars: standing = no service, sitting = service in bill, round up to nearest EUR1, tip jar EUR0.10-0.50; taxis not expected (EUR1/bag if helps); hotel porter ~EUR1/bag, concierge EUR5-10, cleaning ~EUR2/day; hair/massage service included, EUR2-5 for special; tour guide EUR5 half day / EUR10 full day. CANADA — Calculator.net U.S. and Canada table (15-20% restaurants etc., already cited on the tip hub) + Research Co. survey (Dec 10-12 2022, 1,000 adults, +/-3.1 pts, published Dec 30 2022): good service at a busy sit-down restaurant: 41% say 15-19%, 19% say 20%+; average service: 41% say 10-14%, 28% say 15-19%; delivery: 40% say 10-14% acceptable, 11% say no tip OK; takeout: 54% say a tip is not necessary. CRA tax table (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html, modified Apr 1 2025): GST/HST + PST — AB 5+0, BC 5+7, MB 5+7, NB 15 HST, NL 15 HST, NT 5+0, NS 14 HST (cut from 15 on Apr 1 2025), NU 5+0, ON 13 HST, QC 5+9.975, PE 15 HST, SK 5+6, YT 5+0. Some provinces tax restaurant meals/alcohol differently: the tool must say "use the tax line on your receipt to be exact".
