How to convert weight

Weight units are all defined from the kilogram. The pound is exactly 0.45359237 kg and the ounce is exactly 1/16 of a pound, 28.349523 g. To go from pounds to kilograms multiply by 0.45359237: 150 lb = 68.038855 kg. To go the other way multiply by 2.2046226: 70 kg = 154.32358 lb.

The formula

lb = kg × 2.2046226KG = LB × 0.45359237 · G = OZ × 28.349523 · LB = STONE × 14

Common questions

How many pounds are in a kilogram?

1 kg = 2.204622622 lb (rounded). 70 kg = 154.32358 lb, 100 kg = 220.46226 lb.

How do I convert pounds to kg?

Multiply pounds by 0.45359237, which is exact. 150 lb = 68.038855 kg and 200 lb = 90.718474 kg.

How many ounces are in a pound?

16. 1 lb = 16 oz, and one ounce is 1 oz = 28.349523 g.

How much is a stone in pounds and kg?

One stone is 14 pounds: 1 st = 14 lb = 6.3502932 kg. So 11 stone is 11 st = 69.853225 kg.

What is the difference between a metric ton, a US ton and a long ton?

A metric ton (tonne) is 1,000 kg = 2,204.6226 lb. A US (short) ton is 1 US ton = 2,000 lb, and an imperial (long) ton is 1 long ton = 2,240 lb.
