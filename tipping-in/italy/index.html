Coperto and servizio

Coperto is a cover charge of about €1 to €3 per person. Servizio is a service charge of about 15% to 20%, mostly in tourist areas; if it is on your bill, you do not need to add a tip. If it is not, a tip is optional and rounding up is common for a good meal. Tips are left in cash.

Example: a €86.40 bill for four with €2.00 coperto each includes €8.00 of coperto and €78.40 of food and drink. Rounding up to the next €5 is €90.00, a €3.60 tip.

Common questions about tipping in Italy

Do you tip in Italy?

Tipping is uncommon in Italy; it is mainly for special service. Check the bill first: a service charge (servizio) of about 15% to 20% is often included, and the cover charge (coperto) is not a tip.

What is coperto?

Coperto is a cover charge of about €1 to €3 per person for the table and bread service, listed on the menu. It is not a tip and usually goes to the restaurant. Some places also charge a separate pane (bread) fee of €1 to €2.

What is servizio?

Servizio is a service charge, typically about 15% to 20% of the bill, mostly in tourist areas. If it is on the bill, extra tipping is only needed for exceptional service.

How much do you tip at a restaurant in Italy if there is no servizio?

Optional. Rounding up is common for a good meal. On a €86.40 bill for four with €2.00 coperto each, rounding up to the next €5 means paying €90.00, a €3.60 tip (€22.50 each); to the next €10, €90.00.

Do you tip in bars and cafes in Italy?

If you stand at the bar there is no service fee; sitting down includes service in the bill. For great service, round up to the nearest €1. Tip jars usually take €0.10 to €0.50.

Do you tip taxi drivers and hotel staff in Italy?

Tipping a taxi driver is not expected; consider €1 per bag if the driver helps with luggage. Hotel porters get about €1 per bag, concierges €5 to €10 for significant help, and cleaning staff about €2 per day.

How much do you tip a tour guide in Italy?

For a guide you enjoyed, consider €5 for a half-day tour or €10 for a full day. Volunteer guides on free tours can get €5 to €10.
