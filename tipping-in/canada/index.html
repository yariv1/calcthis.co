How much to tip in Canada

Calculator.net lists 15% to 20% for restaurants and bartenders, 15% to 20% for food delivery, 10% to 20% for hairstylists, barbers, nail services and massage, and 15% to 20% for taxi and limo drivers in the U.S. and Canada. See how much to tip by service for more.

Common questions about tipping in Canada

How much do you tip in Canada?

15% to 20% of the bill is the typical range at restaurants and bars, according to Calculator.net’s table for the U.S. and Canada. In a December 2022 Research Co. survey of 1,000 Canadian adults, 41% said 15% to 19% was an acceptable tip for good service at a busy sit-down restaurant and 19% said 20% or more. On a $100.00 bill, 15% is $15.00, 18% is $18.00 and 20% is $20.00.

Do you tip before or after tax in Canada?

Both are common. In Ontario, where the HST is 13%, a $100.00 meal costs $113.00 with tax; an 18% tip is $18.00 on the pre-tax price and $20.34 on the total — $2.34 more. The calculator picks the tax from your province and shows both.

What is the sales tax in Canada?

It depends on the province. Alberta, the territories and Yukon charge 5% GST; Ontario is 13% HST; Nova Scotia is 14% HST since April 1, 2025; New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador and Prince Edward Island are 15% HST; British Columbia and Manitoba add 7% PST to the 5% GST, Saskatchewan 6% and Quebec 9.975%. The table above lists every province, from the Canada Revenue Agency.

How much do you tip for food delivery in Canada?

In the Research Co. survey, 40% of Canadians said a tip of 10% to 14% is acceptable for delivery and 11% said it is acceptable to skip it. Calculator.net lists 15% to 20% for food delivery in the U.S. and Canada, depending on distance and order size.

Do you tip for takeout in Canada?

Often not: 54% of respondents in the Research Co. survey said a tip is not necessary for takeout pickup.

Is the tax on a restaurant bill the same as the rate in the table?

Not always. The table shows each province’s general rate; some provinces tax restaurant meals and alcohol differently. For an exact split, type the tax from your receipt under Go advanced in the calculator.
