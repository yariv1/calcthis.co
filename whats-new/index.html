What’s new on CalcThis

Every calculator, improvement and correction, newest first. If you find a wrong number, write to help@calcthis.co; fixes to published numbers are listed here as corrections.

November 22, 2026

  • New: T-Test Calculator — one-sample, paired and two-sample (Welch and pooled side by side) t-tests from pasted numbers or summary statistics, with the p-value, confidence interval, effect size, a picture of your data and how many more people it would take.
  • New: Dilution Calculator — concentrate and water for any strength, ratio (1:10, 1 in 10, 10x) or factor, in percent, ppm, mg/mL, molar or fold units, with a flask drawn to scale, the mixing order and the same batch at other ratios.
  • Fixed: on the mg to mL Calculator, the error message under the volume box now sits under the whole row instead of half the width.
  • Changed: the menu group Medication & Dosage is now called Medication & Lab; it holds the mg to mL, dilution and peptide reconstitution calculators.

November 21, 2026

  • New: mg to mL Calculator — type a dose and the strength printed on the label and see the milliliters on a to-scale syringe, with mL to mg, strength, teaspoons, tablespoons and label strengths read on DailyMed.
  • New: a Medication & Dosage group in the menu, with the mg to mL calculator and the peptide reconstitution calculator.

November 20, 2026

  • Improved: the contact address is now help@calcthis.co on every page, and the feedback button and contact form send to it.

November 14, 2026

  • New: Amortization Calculator — a chart of every payment split into principal and interest, ten payment frequencies, extra payments and a full schedule.

November 13, 2026

  • New: Compound Interest Calculator and Z-Score Calculator.

November 12, 2026

  • Improved: the 18 unit-conversion pages (for example cm to inches and kg to lbs) now have a worked example, a mental shortcut with its measured error, common mistakes and a longer reference table. Definitions come from NIST publications read on this date.

November 11, 2026

  • Correction: the Alaska paycheck calculator now withholds the employee unemployment-insurance share (0.50% of wages up to $54,200, at most $271 a year). It was missing before.
  • Improved: the paycheck pages for states without a wage income tax explain what still comes out of pay; the pages for taxed states show take-home pay at the state minimum wage (US Department of Labor table updated November 5, 2026).
  • Improved: the random-number pages for 1-3, 1-10, 1-13 and 1-100 now show the average, the spread and the odds of repeats, calculated exactly.

November 10, 2026

  • New: P-Value Calculator, Confidence Interval Calculator, Seconds to Hours, Minutes to Hours and Seconds to Minutes calculators.

November 9, 2026

  • New: Unit Price Calculator — compare products by price per unit and see the best buy.
  • Improved: twelve existing calculators gained options that other well-known calculators offer.

November 8, 2026

  • New: Markup, Profit Margin, Discount, Commission and Realtor Commission calculators.
  • Improved: money fields show the currency symbol inside the box, and results follow the same symbol and side as the chosen currency.

November 7, 2026

  • New: Caffeine Calculator, Caffeine Half Life, Fiber, Creatine, Vitamin D and Body Recomposition calculators.
  • Improved: a field that depends on another now says so when the other one is empty, instead of staying silent (FFMI, dog food, caffeine half life, fiber, keto, net carbs, calories burned).

November 6, 2026

  • New: Weight Loss Calculator by Date, Weight Loss Percentage, Max Heart Rate, Bulking and FFMI calculators.

November 5, 2026

  • New: Dog Food, Body Surface Area, Intermittent Fasting, Keto, Net Carb and Calories Burned calculators.
