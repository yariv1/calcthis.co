How to use this guide

Find the service in the table for the usual range, or pick it in the calculator to see the tip in dollars for your bill: the low end, your own tip and the high end. Click a service for its own guide with the sources and a table of common prices.

Percent or a flat amount?

Most services are a percentage of the price of the service. Movers and valets are flat amounts: $20 to $50 per mover per day, and $2 to $10 per visit. For splitting a restaurant bill, tipping on the pre-tax amount or rounding up, use the tip calculator.

Where the guides disagree the ranges differ a little, and older guidance can be lower. Amounts are customs for the U.S. and Canada, not rules.

Common questions about tipping

How much should I tip?

It depends on the service. 15% to 20% of the price is typical for restaurants, hairdressers, massage therapists and dog groomers; barbers and tattoo artists are 15% to 25%; movers are $20 to $50 per mover per day; a valet is $2 to $10. Pick the service in the calculator above to see the usual range for your bill.

How do I calculate a tip?

Multiply the price by the tip percentage. 10% is the price with the decimal point moved one place left, 20% is that doubled. On $60.00, 10% is $6.00, 15% is $9.00 and 20% is $12.00.

Is tipping required?

No. Tipping is a custom, not a rule, and amounts vary by place and service. Uber, for example, says tipping is optional and drivers are free to accept or decline tips.

Should I tip before or after tax?

Both are common. On a $108.00 bill with $8.00 of tax, a 20% tip is $20.00 before tax and $21.60 on the total. The tip calculator shows both side by side when you enter the tax.

How much do I tip for a $100 service?

At 15% it is $15.00, at 18% $18.00, at 20% $20.00 and at 25% $25.00.
