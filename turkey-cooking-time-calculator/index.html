How to use the turkey cooking time calculator

Enter the turkey’s weight in pounds or kilograms, choose Whole turkey or Turkey breast and, for a whole bird, stuffed or unstuffed. The result is the USDA roasting time at 325 °F, shown as a range — start checking the temperature at the early end and plan on the late end.

Add your dinner date and time and the calculator works backwards into a plan: when to start thawing, when the turkey goes in the oven, when to start checking, when it comes out and when you carve. How long to cook a 12, 15 or 20 lb turkey

A 12 lb turkey takes 2¾–3 hours unstuffed (3–3½ stuffed). A 15 lb turkey takes 3¾–4¼ hours unstuffed (4–4¼ stuffed), and a 20 lb turkey takes 4¼–4½ hours unstuffed (4¼–4¾ stuffed). Times are a guide; the thermometer decides when the turkey is done.

What changes the roasting time

The USDA lists the variables that make a real turkey differ from the chart: a partially frozen bird takes longer, a stuffed bird takes longer, ovens heat unevenly and are often a little off, dark pans cook faster than shiny ones, a deep pan reduces heat circulation, a foil tent for the whole time slows cooking, a lid or an oven bag speeds it, and a turkey or pan that is too big for the oven blocks the heat. Check with a thermometer — the clock is only an estimate.

Safe temperature and resting

Cook the turkey to a safe minimum internal temperature of 165 °F (74 °C), measured in the innermost part of the thigh, the innermost part of the wing and the thickest part of the breast — and in the center of the stuffing if you stuffed it. Then let it stand about 20 minutes before carving.

Common questions

How long does it take to cook a 15 lb turkey?

A 15 lb unstuffed turkey takes 3¾–4¼ hours at 325 °F (3 h 45 min to 4 h 15 min), and a stuffed one takes 4–4¼ hours, according to the USDA chart. Start checking the temperature at the early end and plan on the late end.

How long does it take to cook a 20 lb turkey?

A 20 lb unstuffed turkey takes 4¼–4½ hours at 325 °F and a stuffed one 4¼–4¾ hours. Anything over 24 lb is beyond the USDA chart, so rely on the thermometer rather than the clock.

How much longer does a stuffed turkey take?

A little longer: a 12 lb turkey takes 2¾–3 hours unstuffed and 3–3½ hours stuffed. The USDA recommends cooking stuffing in a casserole for the best safety, and if you do stuff the bird the stuffing must also reach 165 °F in the center.

What temperature should the oven be, and when is the turkey done?

Set the oven to no lower than 325 °F. The turkey is done when a food thermometer reads at least 165 °F (74 °C) in the innermost part of the thigh, the innermost part of the wing and the thickest part of the breast.

Do I need to preheat the oven for a turkey?

No. The USDA says preheating is not necessary for roasting a turkey. Put it in a shallow roasting pan 2 to 2½ inches deep, breast side up on a flat wire rack, and set the oven to 325 °F or higher.

How long should a turkey rest before carving?

About 20 minutes. The USDA recommends letting the bird stand 20 minutes before removing the stuffing and carving so the juices set and the turkey carves more easily. The calculator uses 20 minutes unless you change it under Go advanced.

How long does it take to deep fry a turkey?

The USDA suggests 3 to 5 minutes per pound for a turkey of 8 to 12 lb, with the oil kept at 350 °F — a 12 lb turkey is 36 min to 1 h. Do not stuff a fried turkey.

How long does it take to grill or smoke a turkey?

On a covered grill (gas or charcoal) the USDA suggests 15 to 18 minutes per pound for 8 to 16 lb turkeys — a 12 lb turkey is 3 h to 3 h 36 min — with the air at 225 to 300 °F. A smoker takes 20 to 30 minutes per pound for 8 to 12 lb, so a 10 lb turkey is 3 h 20 min to 5 h. Do not stuff either.

Can I cook a turkey from frozen, and how long does it take?

Yes — an unstuffed turkey of 8 to 24 lb can go into the oven or an electric roaster frozen, and the USDA adds 50% to the cooking time. A 15 lb turkey that takes 3¾–4¼ hours thawed takes about 5 h 38 min to 6 h 23 min from frozen. Do not use an oven cooking bag, and take the giblets out as it thaws.

How long does a partly stuffed turkey take?

The USDA only publishes unstuffed and stuffed times, so partly stuffed is the midpoint: a 12 lb turkey takes about 2 h 53 min to 3 h 15 min. Check the thermometer in the thigh, wing, breast and the center of the stuffing.

How long should I thaw a frozen turkey first?

In the refrigerator allow roughly 24 hours for every 4 to 5 pounds — a 15 lb turkey needs 3 to 3.8 days. In cold water allow about 30 minutes per pound. The turkey thawing time calculator plans it for you.
