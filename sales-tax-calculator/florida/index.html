    Florida sales tax at a glance

    Florida’s state rate is 6%. There are no city sales taxes: the local part is the county’s discretionary sales surtax, 0.5% to 2%, charged where the item is delivered or handed to you. In the Department of Revenue’s 2026 list the combined rate runs from 6% (Citrus and Collier counties) to 8% (Hamilton County). The surtax applies only to the first $5,000 of a single item, so a big purchase costs less than the county rate suggests.

    Orlando (Orange County) is 6.5%, Miami 7%, Tampa 7.5% and Jacksonville 7.5%. Groceries and medicine are exempt but clothing is not, so the calculator looks up your county, your item type and the $5,000 limit instead of guessing.

    What is taxed and what is exempt

    Item typeSales taxDetails
    Most other goods, including clothingTaxableMost goods are taxable, including clothing and shoes (except during the back-to-school holiday). Cosmetics and toiletries stay taxable even with medicinal ingredients, and so do standard stock eyeglasses sold without a prescription.
    Groceries (food for home)ExemptGeneral groceries are exempt, including meat, dairy, produce, bread, drinking water, coffee, vitamins and food supplements. Candy and items advertised as candy, fruit drinks labeled ade, beverage, cocktail or drink, ice cream novelties and ready-to-eat sandwiches are taxable.
    Restaurant and prepared foodTaxableFood prepared and sold for immediate consumption, food sold as part of a prepared meal (hot or cold) and food sold where the entrance has an admission charge are taxable. A bakery with eating facilities charges tax unless the item is taken off the premises.
    Prescription medicine and listed household remediesExemptMedicine dispensed on a prescription is exempt. So are the common household remedies on the Department’s list with or without a prescription — pain relievers, antacids, cough and cold items, first aid supplies — plus insulin, test kits, prescription glasses and contacts and oral hygiene products.
    Baby food, formula, diapers and menstrual productsExemptBaby food and formula, diapers (all ages), incontinence products and menstrual products are exempt.

    Shipping and delivery

    Delivery charges are taxed with a taxable item. They are not taxed only when they are listed separately on the bill and you can avoid them — for example by picking the item up or using your own carrier. If delivery is required by the seller, it is taxed even when listed separately. Untick the box below when you can avoid the charge. On a $100 item in Orange County with $10 shipping the tax is $7.15 when the seller requires delivery and $6.50 when it is avoidable and listed separately.

    Local taxes, special districts and fees

    • County surtax on top of the 6% state tax. Florida’s state rate is 6% everywhere. Each county can add a discretionary sales surtax of 0.5% to 2% by local vote, so the combined rate runs from 6% (Citrus and Collier) to 8% (Hamilton). There are no city or district sales taxes. Search your county or city above.
    • The surtax stops at the first $5,000 of an item. The county surtax applies only to the first $5,000 of the sale of a single item. The 6% state tax applies to the whole price. On a $20,000 item in a county with a 1% surtax the tax is 6% of $20,000 plus 1% of $5,000, not 7% of $20,000. The cap does not apply to prepaid calling arrangements, admissions, transient rentals or services.
    • Which county’s rate applies. The surtax follows the county where the item is delivered or handed to you. For motor vehicles and mobile homes it follows the buyer’s home address, and the calculator does not compute vehicle tax — pick the buyer’s county to see the rate.
    • Hillsborough County. Two of its 0.5% surtaxes were suspended from January 1 to May 31, 2025 and collection resumed on June 1, 2025. The total rate is 7.5%: 6% state plus three 0.5% surtaxes (Department of Revenue TIP 25A01-12).
    • Food, medicine and baby products. Groceries, medicine on the Department’s exempt list and prescription medicine, diapers, baby food and formula and menstrual products are exempt. Candy, restaurant meals, ready-to-eat sandwiches and cosmetics are taxable.
    • Back-to-school sales tax holiday. In 2026 it ran from July 20 to August 20: clothing, footwear and bags of $100 or less per item, school supplies of $50 or less, learning aids and jigsaw puzzles of $30 or less and computers of $1,500 or less for home use were tax free. Florida holds this holiday every year, and the dates are set each year. The calculator does not apply the holiday.
    • Hotels and short-term rentals. Rentals of accommodations for six months or less can pay sales tax plus county transient rental taxes. Those taxes are not included in the calculator.
    • Bought online or out of state. If the seller did not charge Florida tax you owe use tax at the same rate: 6% plus the surtax of the county where you use the item, with the same $5,000 limit. Remote sellers with more than $100,000 of taxable Florida sales in the prior year must collect it. Pick your county and enter any tax the seller charged under Go advanced.
    • Rates change every January. The Department publishes a new county surtax form each November. Surtaxes that end on December 31, 2026 are marked in the county list: Brevard (two 0.5% surtaxes), Charlotte (1%), Liberty (0.5%) and Santa Rosa (0.5%).

    Common questions about Florida sales tax

    What is the sales tax rate in Florida?

    Florida’s state sales tax rate is 6% and each county can add a discretionary sales surtax of 0.5% to 2%, so the combined rate runs from 6% to 8%. In the Department of Revenue’s 2026 list Orange County (Orlando) is 6.5%, Miami-Dade 7%, Hillsborough (Tampa) 7.5%, Duval (Jacksonville) 7.5%, Collier 6% and Hamilton 8%. Florida has no city sales taxes. Search your county or city in the calculator above.

    What is the $5,000 limit on the county surtax?

    The county surtax applies only to the first $5,000 of the sale of a single item, while the 6% state tax applies to the whole price. On a $20,000 item in Miami-Dade County (7%) the tax is $1,250.00 — 6% of $20,000 plus 1% of $5,000 — not $1,400.00. The limit applies per item, not per shopping cart, and does not apply to services, admissions or short-term rentals.

    Is food taxed in Florida?

    Groceries are exempt — meat, dairy, produce, bread, drinking water, coffee, vitamins and food supplements carry no sales tax. Candy, fruit drinks labeled “ade”, “beverage” or “drink”, ice cream novelties and ready-to-eat sandwiches are taxable, and so are restaurant meals: $100 of restaurant food in Orange County has $6.50 of tax. Choose the item type in the calculator to see the difference.

    Is clothing taxed in Florida?

    Yes. Clothing is taxed at the full rate for your county. The exception is the annual back-to-school sales tax holiday — in 2026 it ran from July 20 to August 20 and exempted clothing, shoes and bags of $100 or less per item, school supplies of $50 or less and computers of $1,500 or less for home use. The dates are set each year.

    Are prescription and over-the-counter drugs taxed in Florida?

    Medicine dispensed on a prescription is exempt. The Department of Revenue also lists common household remedies that are exempt with or without a prescription, such as pain relievers, antacids, cough and cold items and first aid supplies, plus insulin, test kits and prescription glasses. Cosmetics and toiletries stay taxable even with medicinal ingredients. Choose “Medicine” as the item type to see the tax removed.

    Is shipping taxed in Florida?

    Delivery charges are taxed with a taxable item unless they are listed separately on the bill and you can avoid them, for example by picking the item up or using your own carrier. On a $100 item in Orange County with $10 shipping the tax is $7.15 when the delivery is required by the seller and $6.50 when it is avoidable and listed separately. Untick the shipping box in the calculator for the avoidable case.

    How do I take sales tax out of a total in Florida?

    Divide the total by 1 plus your county’s rate when the item costs $5,000 or less. In Orange County (6.5%) a total of $106.50 is $100.00 before tax, with $6.50 of tax inside it. Above $5,000 the surtax stops, so the calculator works it out in two steps: a total of $21,250.00 in Miami-Dade County is $20,000.00 before tax. Switch the calculator to “Remove tax”, pick your county and enter the total.

    Do I owe Florida tax on things I buy online?

    Yes, when the seller does not charge Florida tax you owe use tax at the same rate: 6% plus the surtax of the county where you use the item, with the same $5,000 limit. A $3,000 item delivered to Orange County with no tax charged means $195.00 of use tax; if the seller charged 4% you still owe $75.00. Out-of-state sellers with more than $100,000 of taxable Florida sales in the prior year must collect Florida tax. Enter the tax the seller charged under Go advanced.

    Which county’s rate do I pay in Florida?

    The county where the item is delivered or handed to you. For a motor vehicle or a mobile home it is the county of the buyer’s home address, so pick the buyer’s county in the calculator. The calculator does not work out vehicle tax or tourist taxes on hotel stays.
