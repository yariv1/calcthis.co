    Ohio sales tax at a glance

    Ohio’s state rate is 5.75%. Every county adds its own tax and some add a transit tax, and the rate that applies depends on where the sale is sourced — usually the county where you receive the item. In the October 1 – December 31, 2026 table the combined rate runs from 6.5% to 8% by county, and up to 8.25% in the Reynoldsburg part of Licking County. State law caps the combined rate at 8.75%, and rates can only change on January 1, April 1, July 1 or October 1.

    A few city areas on a transit line pay a different rate from the rest of their county — the parts of Columbus, Dublin, Westerville and Reynoldsburg in Delaware, Fairfield, Licking and Union counties (COTA) and the part of Rossford in Wood County (TARTA) — so they are listed as separate lines in the search.

    What is taxed and what is exempt

    Item typeSales taxDetails
    Most goods, including clothing and medicine without a prescriptionTaxableClothing, electronics, furniture, over-the-counter medicine, vitamins and supplements, soft drinks, alcohol and tobacco are taxable.
    Groceries and takeout food (eaten off the premises)ExemptFood bought to eat off the premises is exempt, takeout included. Soft drinks, alcohol, dietary supplements and tobacco are taxable — choose “Other goods” for those.
    Restaurant meals and food eaten on the premisesTaxableFood eaten on the premises, restaurant meals and catered food are taxable.
    Prescription medicine and suppliesExemptPrescription medicine and supplies (insulin, test strips, syringes), doctor-prescribed equipment and prescription glasses and contacts are exempt. Over-the-counter medicine, vitamins and supplements are taxable.
    Baby products and feminine hygiene productsExemptChildren’s diapers, cribs, strollers and car seats that meet safety standards, and pads, tampons and period underwear are exempt.

    Shipping and delivery

    A delivery charge from the seller is part of the price: it is taxed with a taxable item, not taxed with an exempt one, and a mixed shipment is split by price or weight. A carrier you pay directly is not taxed. On a $100 taxable item in Franklin County with $10 shipping the tax is $8.80; on $100 of exempt groceries with $10 shipping it is $0.00.

    Local taxes, special districts and fees

    • County and transit taxes. Every county adds its own tax, and some add a transit tax, to the 5.75% state rate. In the October–December 2026 table the combined rate runs from 6.5% to 8%, and the law caps it at 8.75%. Search your county above.
    • Transit-area city portions. The parts of Columbus, Dublin, Westerville and Reynoldsburg that lie in Delaware, Fairfield, Licking or Union County, and the part of Rossford in Wood County, pay the Central Ohio Transit Authority (COTA) or Toledo Area Regional Transit Authority (TARTA) rate, up to 8.25%. They are separate lines in the search.
    • Food. Food bought to eat off the premises is exempt, takeout included. Soft drinks, alcohol, dietary supplements and tobacco are taxable, and so is food eaten on the premises.
    • Medicine and medical items. Prescription medicine and supplies, doctor-prescribed equipment and prescription glasses and contacts are exempt. Over-the-counter medicine, vitamins and supplements are taxable.
    • Baby and period products. Children’s diapers, cribs, strollers, car seats and feminine hygiene products are exempt.
    • Back-to-school sales tax holiday. In 2026 it ran from 12:00 a.m. Friday, August 7 to 11:59 p.m. Sunday, August 9: clothing of $75 or less, school supplies of $20 or less and school instructional materials of $20 or less per item were exempt. The dates and limits are set each year. The calculator does not apply the holiday.
    • Delivery charges. A delivery charge the seller adds is taxed when the item is taxable. If a shipment mixes taxable and exempt items the charge is split by price or weight; if the seller does not split it, the whole charge is taxed.
    • Bought online or out of state. If the seller did not charge Ohio tax you owe Ohio use tax at your county’s rate. Pick your county and enter any tax the seller charged under Go advanced.
    • Rates change every quarter. County and transit rates can only change on January 1, April 1, July 1 or October 1. Warren County rose from 6.75% to 7.25% on October 1, 2026; the rates here are from the October–December 2026 table.

    Common questions about Ohio sales tax

    What is the sales tax rate in Ohio?

    Ohio’s state sales tax rate is 5.75% and every county adds its own tax, and some add a transit tax. In the October–December 2026 table the combined rate runs from 6.5% (Butler, Lorain, Stark and Wayne counties) to 8% (Cuyahoga and Franklin) — up to 8.25% in the Reynoldsburg part of Licking County. Hamilton County is 7.8%, Lucas 7.75% and Summit 6.75%. State law caps the combined rate at 8.75%. Search your county in the calculator above.

    Why is Ohio sales tax different in every county?

    Counties and regional transit authorities can add tax in steps of 0.05%, so each county ends up with its own rate. A $100 purchase costs $108.00 in Franklin County (Columbus) but $106.50 in Butler County. Rates can only change on January 1, April 1, July 1 or October 1 — Warren County went from 6.75% to 7.25% on October 1, 2026.

    Is food taxed in Ohio?

    Food you eat off the premises is exempt, takeout included: a $100 grocery bill has $0.00 of tax. Soft drinks, alcohol, dietary supplements and tobacco are taxable even in a grocery store, and food eaten on the premises is taxable — $100 of restaurant food in Franklin County has $8.00 of tax. Choose the item type in the calculator to see the difference.

    Is clothing taxed in Ohio?

    Yes. Clothing is taxed at your county’s rate — $8.00 on $100 in Franklin County. The exception is the back-to-school sales tax holiday: in 2026 it ran from Friday, August 7 to Sunday, August 9 and exempted clothing of $75 or less per item. The dates and limits are set each year.

    Are prescription drugs taxed in Ohio?

    No. Prescription medicine and supplies, doctor-prescribed equipment and prescription glasses and contacts are exempt. Over-the-counter medicine, vitamins and supplements are taxable. Choose “Prescription” as the item type to see the tax removed.

    Is shipping taxed in Ohio?

    A delivery charge the seller adds is part of the price, so it is taxed with a taxable item and not taxed with an exempt one. On a $100 taxable item in Franklin County with $10 shipping the tax is $8.80; on $100 of exempt groceries it is $0.00. A mixed shipment is split by price or weight, and a carrier you pay directly is not taxed.

    How do I take sales tax out of a total in Ohio?

    Divide the total by 1 plus your county’s rate. In Franklin County (8%) a total of $108.00 is $100.00 before tax, with $8.00 of tax inside it. Switch the calculator to “Remove tax”, pick your county and enter the total.

    Do I owe Ohio tax on things I buy online?

    Yes, when the seller does not charge Ohio tax you owe use tax at your county’s rate. A $3,000 item delivered to Franklin County with no tax charged means $240.00 of use tax; if the seller charged 5% you still owe $90.00. Enter the tax the seller charged under Go advanced.
