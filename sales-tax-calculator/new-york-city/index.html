    NYC sales tax at a glance

    The combined rate is 8.875%: 4% New York State, 4.5% New York City and 0.375% for the Metropolitan Commuter Transportation District (MCTD). It is the same in Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx and Staten Island. On $100 that is $8.88 of tax, for a total of $108.88.

    The $110 clothing rule

    Clothing and footwear that cost less than $110 per item or pair are exempt from all three parts of the tax. The test is the price of each item, not the total of the purchase. Cross the line and the full tax applies to the whole price of that item.

    Shipping and delivery

    Shipping, handling and delivery charged by the seller on a taxable item are taxable. If you arrange delivery yourself and pay the carrier directly, the carrier’s charge is not taxed. Delivery of an exempt item (groceries, clothing under $110) is not taxed. On a $100 taxable item with $10 shipping the tax is $9.77.

    Other taxes and special rates

    • One rate in all five boroughs. Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx and Staten Island all charge 8.875%: 4% New York State, 4.5% New York City and 0.375% for the Metropolitan Commuter Transportation District (MCTD). The rate follows where the item is delivered or handed to you.
    • Clothing under $110. Clothing and footwear that cost less than $110 per item or pair are exempt from all of the tax. The test is per item, not per purchase: five shirts at $60 each are all exempt, a single $110 jacket pays the full 8.875% on the whole $110.
    • Food and drink. Groceries are exempt. Candy, soft drinks, alcohol, sandwiches, heated food and restaurant meals are taxable. Choose the item type in the calculator to see the difference.
    • Parking. Parking, garaging and storing a car in Manhattan pays 10.375% plus an 8% surtax: 18.375%. A qualifying Manhattan resident with a Manhattan-registered vehicle pays 10.375%.
    • Haircuts, massage and gyms. Beauty and barbering services, massage, tanning and fitness clubs pay the 4.5% City tax on the service only. Products sold there pay 8.875%.
    • Hotels, tickets and other taxes. Hotel occupancy tax, admission and ticket taxes and similar local taxes are not computed here. Check the NYC Department of Finance for those.
    • Bought online or out of state. If the seller did not charge New York tax you owe use tax at the same rate where you use the item. A credit may be available for tax paid to another state; enter the rate the seller charged under Go advanced.

    Common questions about NYC sales tax

    What is the sales tax rate in New York City?

    8.875% in all five boroughs — Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx and Staten Island. It is 4% New York State, 4.5% New York City and 0.375% for the Metropolitan Commuter Transportation District (MCTD). On a $100 purchase the tax is $8.88 and the total is $108.88; on $1,000 it is $88.75, for $1,088.75.

    Is clothing taxed in NYC?

    Only when a single item costs $110 or more. Clothing and footwear under $110 per item or pair are exempt from the state, city and MCTD tax. The test is per item: five shirts at $60 each cost $300 with $0.00 of tax, but one $110 jacket costs $119.76, because the full 8.875% ($9.76) applies to the whole price. A $109.99 item has $0.00 of tax. Jewelry, watches, costumes, rented formalwear, athletic equipment and protective gear are not exempt.

    Are groceries taxed in New York City?

    No. Food, food products, dietary foods and health supplements sold for home use are exempt. Candy, soft drinks, alcohol, fruit drinks with less than 70% juice, sandwiches and heated food are taxable, and so are restaurant meals. Choose the item type in the calculator to see the difference.

    How much is the tax on a restaurant meal in NYC?

    Restaurant food and drink are taxed at the full 8.875%. A $40 meal has $3.55 of tax and costs $43.55 before any tip.

    Is shipping taxed in New York City?

    Shipping, handling and delivery charged by the seller on a taxable item are taxable: a $100 item with $10 shipping has $9.77 of tax. Shipping on an exempt item is not taxed ($0.00 on $100 of groceries with $10 shipping). In a mixed shipment the charge is split by price: $100 of goods and $50 of clothing under $110 with $10 shipping has $9.47 of tax. If you arrange delivery yourself and pay the carrier directly, the carrier’s charge is not taxed.

    How do I take sales tax out of a total in NYC?

    Divide the total by 1.08875. A total of $108.88 is $100.00 before tax, with $8.88 of tax inside it. Switch the calculator to “Remove tax” to do it for any amount. For clothing there is a gap: no clothing purchase can total between $110.00 and $119.76, because an item under $110 has no tax and an item at $110 or more costs at least $119.76.

    What is the sales tax on parking in NYC?

    Parking, garaging or storing a vehicle in Manhattan pays 10.375% plus an 8% surtax, 18.375%: $9.19 of tax on a $50 garage fee. A Manhattan resident with a Manhattan-registered vehicle who qualifies pays 10.375%: $5.19 on $50.

    Are haircuts, massages and gym memberships taxed in NYC?

    The city taxes these services at 4.5% on the service itself, with no New York State tax: a $60 haircut has $2.70 of tax. Products you buy there, such as shampoo, pay the full 8.875%.

    Do I owe New York tax on things I buy online?

    Yes, when the seller does not charge New York tax you owe use tax at the rate where you use the item. A $3,000 item with $50 shipping and no tax charged means $270.69 of use tax. A credit may be available for tax you paid to another state: with a $4,000 purchase and 5.5% paid elsewhere the calculator shows $135.00 still owed. Enter the rate the seller charged under Go advanced.
