    Virginia sales tax at a glance

    Virginia’s rate depends on the locality: 5.3% in most places, 6% in Northern Virginia, Hampton Roads and Central Virginia (a 0.7% regional tax), 6.3% in eight localities that added 1% and 7% in James City County, York County and Williamsburg. The rate that applies is the one for the locality where the sale is made.

    Food for home consumption and essential personal hygiene products pay a reduced 1% rate everywhere. Richmond is 6%, Virginia Beach 6%, Fairfax County 6% and Roanoke 5.3%, so the calculator looks up your exact locality instead of guessing.

    What is taxed and what is exempt

    Item typeSales taxDetails

    Groceries (food for home)TaxableFood for home consumption pays a reduced 1% rate everywhere in Virginia. Most staple groceries and cold prepared food packaged for home qualify; alcohol, tobacco and prepared hot food for immediate consumption do not.
    Feminine hygiene and incontinence productsTaxablePads, tampons, menstrual cups and other menstrual products, and diapers and other nondurable incontinence products, pay the same reduced 1% rate as groceries.
    Prepared hot food and restaurant mealsTaxablePrepared hot food packaged for immediate consumption on or off the premises, and food from caterers, concessions and similar sellers, pays your locality’s full rate.
    Prescription and nonprescription drugsExemptPrescription drugs and nonprescription drugs and medicines are exempt. Dietary supplements and vitamins sold as such are not.

    Shipping and delivery

    A transportation or delivery charge listed separately on the invoice is not taxed. A combined “shipping and handling” charge is taxable even when it is listed as one line, and a charge not listed separately becomes part of the price — tick the box in the calculator for those cases. On a $100 item in Richmond with $10 shipping the tax is $6.00 when the shipping is listed separately and $6.60 when it is billed as shipping and handling.

    Local taxes, special districts and fees

    • The rate depends on the locality. Virginia charges 5.3% in most places. Northern Virginia, Hampton Roads and Central Virginia add a 0.7% regional tax (6%), eight localities that added a 1% local tax pay 6.3% (Charlotte, Gloucester, Halifax, Henry, Northampton, Patrick and Pittsylvania counties and Danville), and James City County, York County and Williamsburg pay 7% with the 1% Historic Triangle tax. Search your locality above.
    • Groceries and hygiene products at 1%. Food for home consumption and essential personal hygiene products (feminine hygiene products and nondurable incontinence products) pay a reduced 1% rate throughout Virginia. Alcohol, tobacco, prepared hot food for immediate consumption and seeds and plants for growing food do not qualify. A seller whose immediate-consumption food sales are 80% or more of its sales cannot charge the reduced rate.
    • Medicine. Prescription drugs and nonprescription drugs and medicines are exempt; dietary supplements and vitamins sold as such are taxable.
    • Delivery charges. A separately stated transportation or delivery charge on a taxable sale is not taxed (23 VAC 10-210-6000). A combined “shipping and handling” charge is taxable even if it is one separate line, and so is a delivery charge that is not listed separately.
    • Cars, boats and aircraft. Motor vehicles, aircraft and watercraft are taxed at different rates, through the DMV for vehicles. The calculator does not compute them.
    • Plastic bag tax. Some localities add a 5 cent tax per disposable plastic bag at checkout. It is not a sales tax and is not included.
    • Bought online or out of state. If the seller did not charge Virginia tax you owe use tax at the rate of the locality where you use the item. Pick your locality and enter any tax the seller charged under Go advanced.
    • Rates change. Localities and regions change their rates by law, usually on January 1 or July 1. The rates here are from the Virginia Tax rate sheet for January 2026 to the present.

    Common questions about Virginia sales tax

    What is the sales tax rate in Virginia?

    Most of Virginia pays 5.3%. Northern Virginia, Hampton Roads and Central Virginia pay 6% (Richmond, Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Arlington and Fairfax among them), eight localities pay 6.3% (Charlotte, Gloucester, Halifax, Henry, Northampton, Patrick and Pittsylvania counties and Danville) and James City County, York County and Williamsburg pay 7%. Roanoke is 5.3%. Search your locality in the calculator above.

    Why is the Virginia rate 6% in some places and 5.3% in others?

    Northern Virginia, Hampton Roads and Central Virginia add a 0.7% regional tax, taking the combined rate from 5.3% to 6%. A $100 purchase costs $105.30 in Roanoke and $106.00 in Richmond. The Historic Triangle (James City County, York County and Williamsburg) adds a further 1%: $107.00 in Williamsburg.

    Is food taxed in Virginia?

    Food for home consumption pays a reduced 1% rate everywhere in Virginia, with no regional or additional local tax, so a $100 grocery bill has $1.00 of tax in any locality. Alcohol, tobacco and prepared hot food for immediate consumption do not qualify and pay the full rate: $6.00 on $100 of restaurant food in Richmond. Feminine hygiene and nondurable incontinence products get the same 1% rate.

    Is clothing taxed in Virginia?

    The Virginia Tax pages we used list no clothing exemption, so clothing is calculated at your locality’s full rate — $6.00 on $100 in Richmond and $5.30 in Roanoke.

    Are prescription and over-the-counter drugs taxed in Virginia?

    No. Prescription drugs and nonprescription drugs and medicines are exempt, but dietary supplements and vitamins sold as such are taxable. Choose “Medicine” as the item type to see the tax removed.

    Is shipping taxed in Virginia?

    A transportation or delivery charge listed separately on the invoice is not taxed. A combined “shipping and handling” charge is taxable even when it is on its own line, and so is a charge not listed separately. On a $100 item in Richmond with $10 shipping the tax is $6.00 when the shipping is listed separately and $6.60 when it is billed with handling. Tick the shipping box for the second case.

    How do I take sales tax out of a total in Virginia?

    Divide the total by 1 plus your locality’s rate. In Richmond (6%) a total of $106.00 is $100.00 before tax, with $6.00 of tax inside it. Switch the calculator to “Remove tax”, pick your locality and enter the total.

    Do I owe Virginia tax on things I buy online?

    Yes, when the seller does not charge Virginia tax you owe use tax at the rate of the locality where you use the item. A $3,000 item used in Richmond with no tax charged means $180.00 of use tax; if the seller charged 4% you still owe $60.00. Enter the tax the seller charged under Go advanced.

    What sales tax do I pay on a car in Virginia?

    Motor vehicles, aircraft and watercraft are taxed at different rates (through the DMV for vehicles), so the calculator does not compute vehicle tax.
