    Los Angeles sales tax at a glance

    The California Department of Tax and Fee Administration lists the City of Los Angeles and Los Angeles County at 10.25% in its table effective October 1, 2026: the 7.25% statewide base plus 3% of voter-approved district taxes. On $100 that is $10.25 of tax, for a total of $110.25.

    Rates follow the address of the sale, so a store in a neighbouring city can charge more: Santa Monica and Culver City are 11.25%, Long Beach, Pasadena, Burbank and Glendale 11%, Inglewood 10.75% and Beverly Hills 10.25%. The California sales tax calculator lists every city.

    Shipping and delivery

    A delivery charge that is listed separately on the bill and covers the actual cost of carrier, mail or the seller’s own delivery after the sale is not taxed. A combined “shipping and handling” charge is taxed except for the actual shipping part — tick the box in the calculator to tax it in full. On a $100 item with $10 shipping listed separately the tax is $10.25; billed as one shipping-and-handling charge it is $11.28.

    Other taxes and special rates

    • One rate across the City of Los Angeles. The CDTFA lists the City of Los Angeles and Los Angeles County at 10.25% since October 1, 2026: the 7.25% statewide base plus 3% of voter-approved district taxes. Rates can differ by address, so a store outside the city limits can charge another rate.
    • Nearby cities charge more. In the same CDTFA table Santa Monica and Culver City are 11.25%, Long Beach, Pasadena, Burbank and Glendale 11%, Inglewood 10.75% and Beverly Hills 10.25%. The state calculator for California lists every city.
    • Groceries and drinks. Groceries, candy, juice and noncarbonated bottled water are exempt. Carbonated drinks and alcohol bought in a store are taxable, and so is hot prepared food and restaurant food, whether eaten there or taken away.
    • Clothing and medicine. Clothing has no exemption in California. Medicine dispensed on a prescription and certain medical devices are exempt; over-the-counter medicine is taxable.
    • Delivery charges. Separately stated carrier or mail transport charges are not taxed. If shipping and handling are billed as one amount, only the actual shipping cost can be left out of the tax.
    • Cars. Vehicles are taxed at the rate of the place where they are registered, not where they are bought. The calculator does not apply that rule.
    • Bought online or out of state. If the seller did not charge California tax you owe use tax at the rate where you use the item. Enter the rate the seller charged under Go advanced.

    Common questions about Los Angeles sales tax

    What is the sales tax rate in Los Angeles?

    10.25% in the City of Los Angeles since October 1, 2026, according to the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration (CDTFA). It is the 7.25% statewide base plus 3% of district taxes. On a $100 purchase the tax is $10.25 and the total is $110.25; on $1,000 it is $102.50, for $1,102.50.

    Is the Los Angeles sales tax 9.75% or 10.25%?

    The CDTFA table effective October 1, 2026 lists the City of Los Angeles and Los Angeles County at 10.25%. Some websites still show the earlier 9.75%, so check the date of the rate they quote.

    Why is the sales tax different in Santa Monica or Long Beach?

    Each city can have its own district taxes on top of the 7.25% base. In the same CDTFA table Santa Monica and Culver City are 11.25% and Long Beach, Pasadena, Burbank and Glendale are 11%, so $100 costs $111.25 in Santa Monica and $110.25 in the City of Los Angeles. The rate follows the address of the sale.

    Are groceries taxed in Los Angeles?

    Groceries are exempt — a $100 grocery bill has $0.00 of tax, and candy, juice and noncarbonated bottled water count as groceries. Hot prepared food and restaurant meals are taxable whether eaten there or taken away: a $40 meal has $4.10 of tax. Carbonated drinks and alcohol bought in a store are taxable too ($10.25 on $100).

    Is clothing taxed in Los Angeles?

    Yes. California has no clothing exemption, so clothing and shoes are taxed at 10.25% — $10.25 on $100.

    Is shipping taxed in Los Angeles?

    Not when the charge is listed separately on the bill and covers the actual cost of carrier, mail or the seller’s own delivery after the sale (CDTFA Regulation 1628). On a $100 item with $10 shipping listed separately the tax is $10.25; billed as one shipping-and-handling charge it is $11.28, because only the actual shipping part can be left out. Tick the box in the calculator for the combined case.

    How do I take sales tax out of a total in Los Angeles?

    Divide the total by 1.1025. A total of $110.25 is $100.00 before tax, with $10.25 of tax inside it. Switch the calculator to “Remove tax” to do it for any amount.

    Do I owe tax on things I buy online?

    Yes, when the seller does not charge California tax you owe use tax at the rate where you use the item. A $3,000 item used in Los Angeles with no tax charged means $307.50 of use tax; if the seller charged 4% you still owe $187.50. Enter the rate the seller charged under Go advanced.

    What sales tax do I pay on a car in Los Angeles?

    Vehicles are taxed at the rate of the place where they will be registered, not where they are bought. For a $30,000 car registered in the City of Los Angeles the tax at 10.25% is $3,075.00. Registration fees are separate. The car sales tax calculator applies California’s trade-in and rebate rules.
