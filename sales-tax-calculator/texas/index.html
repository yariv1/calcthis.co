    Texas sales tax at a glance

    Texas’s state rate is 6.25%. Cities, counties, transit authorities and special districts add up to 2% on top, so the combined rate is capped at 8.25%. In the Comptroller’s October 2026 table the combined rate runs from 6.5% to 8.25% — 8.25% in most big cities and 6.75% in many small towns. Local rates can change in January, April, July and October.

    Houston, Dallas, Austin and San Antonio are all 8.25%. Groceries, water, milk and medicine are exempt, but candy, soft drinks, restaurant food and clothing are taxed — so the calculator looks up your place and item type instead of guessing.

    What is taxed and what is exempt

    Item typeSales taxDetails
    Most other goods, including clothingTaxableMost goods are taxable, including clothing and shoes (except during the August sales tax holiday), paper, pet and beauty products, and books.
    Groceries (food for home)ExemptBasic groceries such as flour, sugar, bread, milk, eggs and produce are exempt. Water, milk and juice drinks with more than 50% juice are exempt too. Candy, gum, soft drinks and individual-size snacks are taxable — choose “Candy, soda & snacks”.
    Restaurant meals and prepared foodTaxableMeals and food prepared or heated by the seller are taxable, whether eaten there or taken away.
    Candy, gum, soft drinks and individual snacksTaxableCandy and gum, soft drinks and individual-size snacks (under 2.5 oz or labeled as one serving) are taxable, even in a grocery store. Multipack snacks are not.
    Prescription and over-the-counter medicineExemptPrescription drugs and over-the-counter medicine with an FDA Drug Facts label are exempt. A drug without a Drug Facts label is exempt only when a physician prescribes or dispenses it.

    Shipping and delivery

    Delivery and shipping charges connected with a taxable item are taxable and are not taxed with an exempt item. A mixed shipment is split by price. On a $100 taxable item in Austin with $10 shipping the tax is $9.08; on $100 of exempt groceries with $10 shipping it is $0.00.

    Local taxes, special districts and fees

    • The rate depends on the place. Texas’s state rate is 6.25%. Cities, counties, transit authorities and special districts add up to 2%, so the combined rate is capped at 8.25%. In the October 2026 table the combined rate runs from 6.5% to 8.25%: 8.25% in most big cities, 6.75% in many small towns and unincorporated areas. Search your place above.
    • Where the rate comes from. In person, the local rate is the one where you buy. For an order shipped to you, the Comptroller’s rule depends on where the seller received and filled it: a seller with a Texas place of business generally charges its own location’s local rate, a seller shipping from a Texas non-business location charges the ship-to rate, and an order filled from outside Texas is taxed at the ship-to (delivery) address.
    • Cities in more than one county. A city that sits in more than one county or district is listed once for each county, because the pieces that make up its rate differ. Pick the county where the sale happens; if the county variants all give the same total the calculator lists the city once.
    • Food and drink. Groceries, water, milk and juice drinks over 50% juice are exempt. Candy and gum, soft drinks and individual-size snacks are taxable, and so is restaurant food. Choose the item type to see the difference.
    • Clothing and the August tax-free weekend. Clothing is taxable at your place’s rate, except during the annual sales tax holiday in August, when most clothing and footwear sold for under $100 is tax free. The next one is August 6–8, 2027. The calculator does not apply the holiday.
    • Medicine. Prescription drugs and over-the-counter medicine with an FDA Drug Facts label are exempt.
    • Delivery charges. Delivery and shipping charges connected with a taxable item are taxable — on a $500 sofa with $50 delivery the tax is charged on $550. Delivery of an exempt item is not taxed.
    • Cars. Motor vehicles pay the state motor vehicle sales tax of 6.25% to the county tax office; local sales taxes are not charged on them. The calculator does not compute vehicle tax.
    • Bought online or out of state. If the seller did not charge Texas tax you owe use tax: 6.25% state plus up to 2% local use tax depending on where you use the item, less any sales tax already paid to another state. Pick your place and enter any tax the seller charged under Go advanced.
    • Rates change every quarter. Local rates can change on January 1, April 1, July 1 or October 1. The rates here are from the October 2026 table.

    Common questions about Texas sales tax

    What is the sales tax rate in Texas?

    Texas’s state sales tax rate is 6.25% and cities, counties, transit authorities and special districts add up to 2%, so the combined rate is capped at 8.25%. In the Comptroller’s October 2026 table the combined rate runs from 6.5% to 8.25%: Houston, Dallas, Austin and San Antonio are all 8.25%, while a small town such as Ace is 6.75%. Search your city in the calculator above for your rate.

    Why does Texas sales tax differ from town to town?

    Each place adds its own pieces to the 6.25% state rate — a city tax (up to 2%), a county tax, a transit authority or a special district — and the total can never go above 8.25%. A $100 purchase costs $108.25 in Austin but $106.75 in Ace. A city that spans several counties is listed once for each county where the pieces differ.

    Is food taxed in Texas?

    Basic groceries are exempt: a $100 grocery bill has $0.00 of tax, and water, milk and juice drinks with more than 50% juice are exempt too. Candy and gum, soft drinks and individual-size snacks (under 2.5 ounces) are taxable even in a grocery store — $8.25 on $100 in Austin. Restaurant food is taxable: $8.25 on $100. Choose the item type in the calculator to see the difference.

    Is clothing taxed in Texas?

    Yes. Clothing is taxed at your place’s rate — $8.25 on $100 in Austin. The exception is the annual sales tax holiday in August, when most clothing and footwear sold for under $100 is tax free; the next one is August 6–8, 2027.

    Are prescription and over-the-counter drugs taxed in Texas?

    No. Prescription drugs and over-the-counter medicine with an FDA Drug Facts label are exempt. A drug without a Drug Facts label is exempt only when a physician prescribes or dispenses it. Choose “Medicine” as the item type to see the tax removed.

    Is shipping taxed in Texas?

    Delivery and shipping charges connected with a taxable item are taxable, and charges on an exempt item are not. A $500 item with $50 delivery in Austin is taxed on $550 — $45.38 of tax. On a $100 taxable item with $10 shipping the tax is $9.08; on $100 of exempt groceries with $10 shipping it is $0.00.

    How do I take sales tax out of a total in Texas?

    Divide the total by 1 plus your combined rate. In Austin (8.25%) a total of $108.25 is $100.00 before tax, with $8.25 of tax inside it. Switch the calculator to “Remove tax”, pick your city and enter the total.

    Do I owe Texas tax on things I buy online?

    Yes, when the seller does not charge Texas tax you owe use tax: 6.25% state plus up to 2% local use tax, depending on where you use the item, less any sales tax you already paid to another state. A $3,000 item used in Austin with no tax charged means $247.50 of use tax; if the seller charged 4% you still owe $127.50. Enter the tax the seller charged under Go advanced.

    Which local rate applies to an order shipped to me in Texas?

    It depends on where the seller received and filled the order. A seller with a Texas place of business generally charges its own location’s local rate; if the order was filled from a Texas location that is not a place of business, or from outside Texas, the ship-to address decides. For in-person purchases it is the rate where you buy. The Comptroller’s Local Sales and Use Tax Collection publication (94-105) has the full table.

    What sales tax do I pay on a car in Texas?

    Motor vehicles pay the state motor vehicle sales tax of 6.25% to the county tax office, and local sales taxes are not charged on them. The calculator does not compute vehicle tax.
