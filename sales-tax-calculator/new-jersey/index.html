    New Jersey sales tax at a glance

    New Jersey’s statewide rate is 6.625%. On $100 that is $6.63 of tax, for a total of $106.63. The rate comes from the New Jersey Division of Taxation and the Tax Foundation’s July 1, 2026 table.

    Shipping and delivery

    Delivery charges are taxed when the item is taxable and not taxed when it is exempt. A mixed shipment is split by price. On a $100 taxable item with $10 shipping the tax is $7.29.

    Special zones, local taxes and fees

    • Urban Enterprise Zones and Salem County. In-person sales by certified sellers in an Urban Enterprise Zone, and most in-person goods sales in Salem County, are taxed at half the rate: 3.3125%. Online, mail-order, services, prepared food, alcohol and motor vehicles in Salem County pay the full rate. Pick your location above.
    • Atlantic City. A luxury tax of 9% applies to certain sales, rentals and services (3% on alcohol sold by the drink). The state rate is reduced so the combined rate never passes 12.625%.
    • Wildwood, Wildwood Crest and North Wildwood. A 2% tourism tax is added to restaurant food and drink, lodging and admissions: 8.625% in total. Pick your location above.
    • Hotels and short-term rentals. Sales tax plus a state occupancy fee of 5% (1% in Newark, Jersey City and Atlantic City; 3.15% in the three Wildwoods) and up to 3% municipal occupancy tax. These fees are not included in the calculator.
    • Bought online or out of state. If the seller did not charge New Jersey tax, you owe 6.625% use tax on the price plus delivery. If you paid a lower rate elsewhere, you owe the difference — enter that rate under Go advanced.

    Common questions about New Jersey sales tax

    What is the sales tax rate in New Jersey?

    New Jersey has a statewide sales tax rate of 6.625%. There is no city or county sales tax, so the same rate applies everywhere in the state, except for the reduced Urban Enterprise Zone and Salem County rate of 3.3125%, the 2% Cape May tourism tax in the Wildwoods and the Atlantic City luxury tax. On a $100 purchase the tax is $6.63 and the total is $106.63; on $1,000 it is $66.25, for $1,066.25.

    Is clothing taxed in New Jersey?

    No. Clothing and footwear for human use are exempt from New Jersey sales tax at any price. The exemption does not cover fur clothing, accessories, or sports, recreational or protective equipment, which are taxable.

    Are groceries taxed in New Jersey?

    Most food bought in a grocery store is exempt. Candy, soft drinks, alcohol and tobacco are taxable, and so is prepared food — food heated by the seller, combined by the seller or sold with utensils, such as restaurant meals. Choose the item type in the calculator to see the difference.

    Is shipping taxed in New Jersey?

    It depends on what you bought. Delivery charges are taxed when the item is taxable and are not taxed when the item is exempt, such as clothing. On a $100 taxable item with $10 shipping the tax is $7.29, for a total of $117.29. If one shipment mixes taxable and exempt items, the delivery charge is split by price: add $100 of goods and $50 of clothing with $10 shipping and the tax is $7.07.

    How do I work out the price before tax from a total in New Jersey?

    Divide the total by 1.06625. A total of $106.63 comes from a price of $100.00 plus $6.63 of tax. Switch the calculator to “Remove tax” to do it for any amount.

    Do I owe New Jersey tax on things I buy online?

    Yes, when the seller does not charge New Jersey tax you owe 6.625% use tax on the price plus delivery. A $3,000 pool table with $50 shipping and no tax charged means $202.06 of use tax. If the seller charged a lower rate than New Jersey’s, you owe the difference: a $4,000 desk bought with 5.5% tax paid elsewhere leaves $45.00. Enter the rate the seller charged under Go advanced.

    What is the sales tax in Salem County or an Urban Enterprise Zone?

    Certain in-person sales by qualified sellers are taxed at half the rate, 3.3125%. A $100 item in Salem County has $3.31 of tax. Online and mail-order sales, services, prepared food, alcohol and motor vehicles do not get the reduced rate in Salem County.
