How to use it

Enter your date of birth. Your age appears at once in years, months and days — the way official forms ask for it — with a seconds counter ticking underneath. Below that you get totals in months, weeks, days and hours, the day of the week you were born, and how long until your next birthday, including the date and weekday it lands on.

The timeline shows your life so far as a bar running to your next decade birthday, with a marker for today and ticks at each past decade. Tap Go advanced and pick any date to see how old you were — or will be — on that day.

How age in years, months and days is counted

The calculator counts whole years from your birth date to today, then the whole months since your last birthday, then the days left over. Leap years and the different lengths of each month are handled for you, so a date like 31 January to 1 March comes out right rather than off by a day or two.

Why a dedicated tool beats mental math

Working out an exact age by hand means juggling leap years, 28-, 30- and 31-day months, and year boundaries. It's easy to be a day or two out — which matters for visa forms, school-year cut-offs, retirement dates and legal paperwork. This tool gives the same answer every time, instantly.

Age on a specific date

Advanced mode answers "how old was I on…" and "how old will I be on…". Useful for working out your age at a past event, at a future deadline, or on the day a rule kicks in — a driving age, a pension date, an exam cut-off.

Chronological age for school

Schools often record a child's age in years and months for grade placement and assessment scoring, because a few months either way can change the reference group. The years-and-months figure at the top is exactly that number.

Leap-day birthdays

Born on 29 February? In non-leap years the calculator treats your birthday as 1 March for the countdown, which is the most common convention. Your age in years still increases once per calendar year.

Common questions

How is age calculated in years, months and days?

The calculator counts whole years from your birth date to today, then whole months since your last birthday, then the leftover days. Leap years and month lengths are handled, so the result matches how age is stated on official forms.

How do I calculate how many days old I am?

Enter your date of birth and you get your total age in days, plus weeks, months and hours. The days figure is the exact number of calendar days between your birth date and today.

How many days until my next birthday?

The calculator shows the days to your next birthday, the exact date and weekday it falls on, and the age you'll turn. If today is your birthday, it says so.

What day of the week was I born on?

It returns the weekday of your date of birth automatically, and also dates milestones such as when you turn 10,000 days old, with the weekday for each.

How old will I be on a future date?

Open advanced mode and pick any date, past or future. You'll see how old you were or will be that day in years, months and days, plus the total days.
