How to use the caffeine calculator

Choose who it is for, pick a drink and type how much you had, in cups or in ml. Press Add drink for each more. The total, the share of the daily limit and the cups of coffee left update as you type. For a teen or child add the body weight, because their limit is per kilogram.

Daily caffeine limits

Healthy adults: the FDA cites 400 mg a day, about two to three 12-fluid-ounce cups of coffee; Health Canada and EFSA also give 400 mg. At 95 mg per 8 fl oz cup that is 4.2 cups.

Teens and children: 2.5 mg per kg of body weight a day (Health Canada) or 3 mg per kg (EFSA): at 50 kg that is 125 mg or 150 mg. The FDA advises against energy drinks for children and teens.

Pregnant, trying to become pregnant or breastfeeding: ask your health care provider; the FDA gives no single number.

A worked example

Three 8 fl oz cups of brewed coffee: 3 × 95 = 285 mg, 71% of 400 mg, which leaves 115 mg, about 1.2 more cups. For timing, see the caffeine half life calculator.

Common questions

How much caffeine is too much in a day?

For most healthy adults the FDA cites 400 milligrams a day as an amount not generally associated with negative effects, about two to three 12-fluid-ounce cups of coffee. Health Canada and EFSA give the same 400 mg for adults. On this calculator’s averages (95 mg per 8 fl oz cup of brewed coffee) 400 mg is 4.2 such cups.

How much caffeine is in a cup of coffee?

About 95 mg in an 8 fl oz cup of brewed coffee on the USDA average of 40 mg per 100 g, 63 mg in a 1 fl oz shot of espresso, 62 mg in instant coffee and 2 mg in decaf. Real cups vary with the beans, the brew and the size, so switch the amount to ml for any size, or type the mg from the label under “My own amount”.

What is the caffeine limit for teens and children?

Health Canada gives 2.5 mg per kilogram of body weight a day for children and adolescents and EFSA gives 3 mg per kg. For a 50 kg (110 lb) teen that is 125 mg and 150 mg: one 12 fl oz can of cola (32 mg) is 26% of the lower figure, and one 16 fl oz energy drink (151 mg) is 121% of it. The FDA adds that medical experts advise against energy drinks for children and teens. The calculator uses the lower Health Canada number as the limit.

Is caffeine safe during pregnancy or breastfeeding?

The FDA says that if you are pregnant, trying to become pregnant or breastfeeding, you should ask your health care provider whether you need to limit caffeine. This calculator still adds up your drinks if you choose the pregnant or breastfeeding option, but it shows no limit.

How many cups of coffee is 400 mg?

4.2 cups of brewed coffee at 95 mg per 8 fl oz cup. The FDA’s own comparison is about two to three 12-fluid-ounce cups, because a bigger cup holds more.

Does decaf coffee have caffeine?

A little. The USDA lists 1 mg per 100 g for brewed decaf, so 2 mg in an 8 fl oz cup. Add it with the Decaf coffee option if you want it counted.

How do I add a drink that is not in the list?

Choose “My own amount (mg)”, type the caffeine in one serving from the label and the number of servings you had. Energy drinks vary a lot by brand, so the label is more accurate than the typical value.

How long does the caffeine stay in my body?

Roughly half of a dose is gone after 5 hours in a typical non-smoker, but it varies a lot from person to person. The caffeine half life calculator draws the curve for your drinks and shows how much is left at bedtime.
