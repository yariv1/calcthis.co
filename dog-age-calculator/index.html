How to use the dog age calculator

Type your dog’s age in years and months — or, under Go advanced, a date of birth. Then tell the calculator the size: tap Small, Medium, Large or Giant, type a weight, or pick a breed (the breed sets the size from its typical adult weight). If you pick nothing, a medium dog is assumed and the calculator says so.

You get the age in human years from the size chart, the AVMA rule and the UCSD formula side by side, a curve with your dog marked on it and the senior window shaded, the age on the next birthday, and the senior age for that size. Switch to Human → dog to work the other way.

The UCSD epigenetic formula

human age = 16 × ln(dog age) + 31 UC SAN DIEGO · WANG ET AL., CELL SYSTEMS 2020

It was fitted on 104 Labrador retrievers from 0 to 16 years old, so it is an estimate for other breeds. It only works from about two months of age (below that the formula gives zero or less).

Dog years to human years by size

Dogs age quickly in the first two years and then more slowly — and larger dogs age faster after that. Size is by adult weight: small up to 20 lb (9 kg), medium 21–50 lb, large 51–90 lb, giant over 90 lb (41 kg).

When is a dog a senior?

About 8–11 years for a small dog, 8–10 medium, 8–9 large and 6–7 giant. The calculator shades that window on the curve.

Common questions

How do I calculate my dog’s age in human years?

Use a size-based chart rather than multiplying by 7. The first year of a dog’s life counts as about 15 human years and the second as about 9 more (24 at age 2); after that each year adds roughly 4 to 7 human years depending on size. A 10-year-old dog is about 56 in human years if small, 60 if medium, 66 if large and 78 if giant. Enter the age and size above and the calculator does it, and also shows the UCSD formula and the AVMA rule.

Is one dog year really 7 human years?

No. The “×7” rule is a rough average that is wrong at both ends: a 1-year-old dog is closer to 15 in human terms (not 7) and a 10-year-old small dog is about 56 (not 70). The calculator shows the ×7 figure next to the real ones so you can see the gap.

Does my dog’s size change the answer?

Yes — large and giant dogs age faster after the first two years. At 10 years a small dog is about 56, a medium dog 60, a large dog 66 and a giant dog 78 human years. Pick a size button, type a weight (the chart uses up to 20 lb small, 21–50 medium, 51–90 large, over 90 giant) or choose a breed.

What is the UCSD dog age formula?

Researchers at UC San Diego measured DNA methylation in 104 Labrador retrievers aged 0 to 16 years and compared it with human methylation. Their formula is human age = 16 × ln(dog age) + 31, which gives 57 for a 5-year-old and 68 for a 10-year-old. It was fitted on Labradors only, so for other breeds it is an estimate; the calculator shows it beside the size chart.

How old is a 1-year-old dog in human years?

About 15 on the size chart and the AVMA rule; the UCSD formula says 31. A 2-year-old is about 24 on the chart and 42 on the UCSD formula, which is why the two methods are shown together.

When is a dog considered a senior?

The AVMA, using AKC data, puts senior age at about 8–11 years for small dogs, 8–10 for medium, 8–9 for large and 6–7 for giant breeds. The calculator shows the senior window on the curve and how long until your dog reaches it.

How do I work out my dog’s age from a birthday?

Open Go advanced and pick or type the date of birth; the age in years and months fills in. If you adopted a dog and only know the approximate age, type the years and months instead.

Can I convert a human age to dog years?

Yes. Switch to “Human → dog” at the top and enter the human age. A medium dog is 37 in human years at 5 and 60 at 10, so a 60-year-old equivalent is a 10-year-old medium dog; the calculator reads the chart in reverse.

How accurate is a dog age calculator?

Every calculator is an estimate: breed, genetics and health change how a dog ages, the size charts are averages, and the UCSD formula comes from one breed. That is why this one shows three methods side by side instead of one number.
