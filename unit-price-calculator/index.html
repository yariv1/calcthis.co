How to use the unit price calculator

Choose what the packs are measured in under Compare by — weight, volume, count, length, area or time. For each product type its price and the amount in one pack, and pick the unit that pack uses: one row can be in ounces and the next in kilograms, the calculator converts. The line under each product shows its price per unit; the result ranks every product cheapest first and shows how much more each of the others costs than the best buy.

Buying cans or bottles? Type the size of one can and put the number of cans in Items in the pack: 6 cans of 330 ml is 1,980 ml. Keep The price is for: The whole pack when the price covers all the cans, or choose One item when the label shows the price of a single can. Show price per picks the unit of the answer, and clicking a row of “The best buy in every unit” switches the whole page to that unit.

Open Go advanced to add servings, a sale percent, a coupon and sales tax to each product, and to type how much you plan to buy — the calculator then rounds up to whole packs and shows what each product really costs for that amount.

The formulas

unit price = price ÷ (amount × items in the pack) PRICE PER CHOSEN UNIT = UNIT PRICE × SIZE OF THAT UNIT

With a sale: price paid = (price × (1 − sale ÷ 100) − coupon) × (1 + tax ÷ 100). Two products from the same example, worked out by this calculator:

Cans, bottles and multipacks

Choose Volume, type 330 ml as the size and the number of cans as the items in the pack. $4.20 for 6 cans is $0.212 per 100 ml; $7.80 for 12 cans is $0.197 per 100 ml, so the bigger pack is cheaper per 100 ml.

Sales, coupons and subscriptions

Under Go advanced the price paid includes the sale, the coupon and tax: a $8.00 pack with 25% off and a $1.00 coupon costs $5.00. Choose Time to compare plans per month, week or day.

Common questions

How do I calculate the unit price?

Divide the price by the amount in the pack. $3.99 for 16 oz is $0.249 per ounce, or $3.99 per pound. In the calculator, type the price and the amount, pick the unit, and the price per unit appears.

How do I compare two products with different sizes or units?

Type each one in its own row with its own unit — one in ounces and the other in pounds or grams works — and the calculator converts them to the same unit. $5.49 for 24 oz costs $0.229 per ounce, so it is 8.3% cheaper per ounce than $3.99 for 16 oz ($0.249).

Can I compare more than two products?

Yes. Add as many products as you like, up to twenty. They are ranked cheapest first, and each bar shows how much more than the best buy it costs.

Is the lowest unit price always the cheapest thing to buy?

Not always. Packs come in whole units. If you need 2 lb (32 oz), two 16 oz packs of Product A cost $7.98 with nothing left over, while Product B, which has the lower price per ounce, needs two 24 oz packs for $10.98 and leaves 16 oz unused. Type the amount you will buy in Go advanced to see this.

How do I compare packs such as 6 cans against 12 cans?

Choose Volume, type the size of one can (330 ml) and the number of cans in “Items in the pack”. $4.20 for 6 × 330 ml is $0.212 per 100 ml; $7.80 for 12 × 330 ml is $0.197 per 100 ml.

How do I include a sale, a coupon or sales tax?

Open Go advanced. Type the sale percent, a coupon amount and the sales tax in the product’s row. The sale comes off first, then the coupon, then tax is added: $8.00 with 25% off and a $1.00 coupon is $5.00.

Can it work out the price per serving?

Yes. In Go advanced type the servings in the pack and each bar shows the price per serving next to the price per unit.

Can I compare subscriptions or rentals?

Yes. Choose Time and type the length of each plan in days, weeks, months or years to see the price per month, week or day. A month is one twelfth of a 365-day year.

Does the currency selector convert my prices?

No. It only changes the symbol and the decimals shown. Type every price in the same currency.
