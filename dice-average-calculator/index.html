How to use the dice average calculator

Enter the number of dice and the sides (tap a chip for d4 to d100), plus an optional modifier such as +3. The big number is the average roll; the chart shows the exact probability of every possible total, with the average marked. Enter a target total to see the chance of rolling exactly that, that or more, and that or less.

Open Go advanced for keep-highest and keep-lowest rules — or tap Advantage, Disadvantage or 4d6 drop low to set them up for you.

The dice average formula

Average of one die = (sides + 1) ÷ 2 AVERAGE OF N DICE = N × (SIDES + 1) ÷ 2, PLUS THE MODIFIER

A d6 averages (6 + 1) ÷ 2 = 3.5, so 2d6 averages 2 × 3.5 = 7 and 2d6+3 averages 10. A d20 averages 10.5, and 3d6 averages 10.5.

Advantage, disadvantage and drop lowest

With advantage you roll 2d20 and keep the higher one; the average rises from 10.5 to 13.825, and the chance of rolling 15 or more goes from 30% to 51%. With disadvantage you keep the lower: the average falls to 7.175. Rolling 4d6 and dropping the lowest averages 12.245 instead of 14 for plain 4d6 — the usual way to roll ability scores.

Why 7 is the most likely roll on 2d6

There are 36 equally likely pairs; six of them add up to 7, but only one makes 2 and one makes 12. So 7 comes up 16.7% of the time, and the chance of 10 or more is 16.7% (6 of 36). The more dice you add, the more the totals bunch into a bell shape around the average — the chart shows it.

The calculator computes the exact odds by counting every combination, not by simulation, so the percentages never wobble between runs. It covers the average, extremes and target odds; it does not cover odds such as "exactly two sixes among five dice". For the average of any list of numbers use the average calculator, or roll for real with the random number generator.

Common questions

How do you calculate the average of dice?

The average of one die is (sides + 1) ÷ 2. Multiply by the number of dice and add the modifier. For 2d6+3: 2 × 3.5 + 3 = 10.

What is the average roll of 2d6?

7. The most likely total is also 7 (16.7% of rolls), and the chance of 10 or more is 16.7%.

What is the average of a d20 with advantage?

13.825 (roll two d20 and keep the higher), compared with 10.5 for a single d20. With disadvantage it is 7.175.

What is the average of 4d6 drop the lowest?

12.245. Plain 4d6 averages 14, and dropping the lowest die removes about 1.76 points on average from the sum of the four.

How do I find the chance of rolling a target number or higher?

Type the target in the Target total box. The calculator counts every combination and shows the chance of exactly that total, that or more, and that or less — for example 10 or more on 2d6 is 16.7%.

Is the dice average the same as the most likely roll?

For most dice sets, yes or very close — the distribution is symmetric. With keep-highest or keep-lowest rules it is not: 2d20 keep highest averages 13.825, but the most likely single total is 20.

How many dice and sides can I use?

Up to 100 dice with up to 100 sides each, plus a modifier. Keep-highest and keep-lowest rules work with up to 12 dice.
